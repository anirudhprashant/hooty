Words this chapter explains

  • netThe unfolded form of a box that can be folded back into a box.
  • prismA solid shape included in the prisms group of the craft section.
  • pyramidA solid shape whose triangular faces meet at a point.
  • faceAn outer part of a 3D shape that may be flat or curved.
  • cornerA point where the parts of a 3D shape meet.
  • edgeA straight side of a 3D shape.
  • opposite faceThe face across from another face, as faces 1 and 6 are.
  • angleThe shape made when two lines meet.
  • right angleAn angle checked with the right angle checker.
  • acute angleAn angle that is less than a right angle.
  • obtuse angleAn angle that is more than a right angle.
  • centreThe middle point from which the radius of a circle is measured.
  • diameterThe length of a straight line passing through the centre of a circle.
