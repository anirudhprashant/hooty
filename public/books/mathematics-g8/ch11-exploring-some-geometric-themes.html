Words this chapter explains

  • fractalA shape that shows a similar pattern again and again at smaller sizes.
  • self-similarHaving the same or a similar pattern at smaller scales.
  • Sierpinski CarpetA fractal made by repeatedly removing the middle square from nine smaller squares.
  • Sierpinski GasketA fractal made by removing the middle triangle from four smaller triangles.
  • Koch SnowflakeA fractal whose sides are repeatedly changed into bump-shaped structures.
  • profileThe outline seen when a solid is viewed from a particular direction.
  • faceA flat surface forming part of the boundary of a solid.
  • edgeA line segment that forms the side of a face.
  • vertexA point at which edges meet.
  • congruentIdentical polygons used as the opposite faces of a prism.
  • netA flat shape that can be folded to make a solid.
  • tetrahedronAnother name for a triangular pyramid.
  • projectionA representation formed by viewing all the points of an object on a plane.
  • front viewThe projection of an object on a vertical plane.
  • isometric projectionA projection in which all the projected edge lengths are equal.
