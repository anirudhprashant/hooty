Words this chapter explains

  • percentageA number that shows how many parts there are in every hundred equal parts.
  • per centThe words used to read and name a percentage symbol.
  • per centumA Latin phrase meaning by the hundred or out of hundred.
  • equivalent fractionTwo fractions that show the same part of a whole.
  • denominatorThe number below the line in a fraction that tells us how many equal parts make one whole.
  • decimalA way of writing a fraction using a point and digits after it.
  • proportionA part or share expressed in relation to the whole.
  • marked priceThe price quoted by a shopkeeper before any discount or bargaining.
  • selling priceThe price that a customer actually pays for an item.
  • cost priceThe price that a shopkeeper pays to buy an item.
  • profitThe money gained when the selling price is above the cost price.
  • lossThe money lost when the selling price is below the cost price.
  • discountA reduction in the quoted price of an item.
  • principalThe deposited amount on which interest is calculated.
  • compoundingAdding earned interest back into a fixed deposit so that it also earns interest.
  • depreciationA reduction in the value of an item because of use and age.
