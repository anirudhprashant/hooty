  • exponential notationA short way of writing repeated multiplication using powers.
  • baseThe number that is repeatedly multiplied in a power.
  • exponentThe number that tells how many times the base is multiplied by itself.
  • powers of 5Numbers made by multiplying 5 by itself different numbers of times.
  • prime factorsThe factors that are prime numbers.
  • counting numbersNumbers used for counting, such as 1, 2, 3, and so on.
  • scientific notationA way of writing a number as a coefficient multiplied by a power of 10.
