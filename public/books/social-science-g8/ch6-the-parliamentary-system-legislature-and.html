Words this chapter explains

  • Bicameral systemThis is a legislature made up of two houses.
  • Unicameral systemThis is a legislature with only one house.
  • Presiding officerThis person ensures that debates in a House remain orderly.
  • BillThis is a proposed law that must be approved before it becomes law.
  • ClauseA clause gives specific details about a proposed law.
  • GazetteThis official publication announces legal documents and government information.
  • Standing CommitteeThis permanent committee examines government work and asks questions.
  • Money BillThis type of bill deals with financial matters.
  • Question HourThis is a special period when MPs question ministers.
  • De factoThis means holding a position in actual practice.
  • Civil servantsThese officials work under ministers to implement laws and policies.
  • FederalismThis is a system where power is shared among Union, state and local governments.
  • Separation of powersThis divides government power among the Legislature, Executive and Judiciary.
  • Checks and balancesThese controls stop any one branch of government from becoming too powerful.
  • State AssemblyThis is a state’s own law-making body.
