Words this chapter explains

  • JudiciaryThe courts and judges that settle disputes and give justice.
  • Judicial systemThe network of courts that handles complaints and disputes.
  • AdjudicationThe formal process of deciding a dispute.
  • Substantive justiceJustice judged by whether the final result is fair and reasonable.
  • Procedural justiceJustice that gives every person a full chance to be heard.
  • Subordinate judiciaryThe lower courts that work below each High Court.
  • JurisdictionThe legal power and area of work given to a court.
  • Writ petitionA legal request made to enforce a constitutional right.
  • Public Interest LitigationA court case that raises an issue affecting the public.
  • Original jurisdictionThe power to hear certain legal disputes first.
  • Appellate jurisdictionThe power to hear appeals from lower court decisions.
  • Advisory jurisdictionThe power to give an opinion on an important question asked by the President.
  • TribunalA special body set up to settle particular kinds of disputes.
  • ArbitrationA way to settle a dispute through an independent arbitrator’s decision.
  • MediationA neutral mediator helps disputing parties agree on a settlement.
