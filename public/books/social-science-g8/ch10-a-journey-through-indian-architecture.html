Words this chapter explains

  • architectureThe art and techniques of designing and constructing buildings.
  • urban planningThe careful organisation of streets, buildings and public services in a city.
  • stupaA dome-shaped Buddhist building that houses sacred relics.
  • toraṇaAn ornamental gateway leading into a sacred enclosure.
  • vedikāA railing or enclosure around a stupa.
  • circumambulateTo walk around a sacred object as part of worship.
  • pradakṣhiṇaGoing clockwise around a sacred object.
  • puṣhkariṇīA water reservoir attached to a temple.
  • garbhagṛihaThe innermost chamber of a temple where the deity’s image is kept.
  • maṇḍapaA pillared hall within a temple.
  • gopuramThe main entrance tower of a south Indian temple.
  • jāliA decorative lattice screen that allows light and air to pass through.
  • minaretA tower from which the muezzin calls people to prayer.
  • cenotaphA monument that marks the place where a person was cremated.
