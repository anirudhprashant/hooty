Words this chapter explains

  • cartographerA person who draws or produces maps.
  • oblate spheroidA nearly round Earth shape that bulges at the equator and is flattened at the poles.
  • map projectionA method of showing the Earth on a flat surface.
  • planktonVery small organisms that use sunlight to make food.
  • ocean currentA river-like movement of warm or cold ocean water.
  • trenchA very deep, V-shaped area of the seafloor.
  • coralA tiny sea animal that can help build a natural reef.
  • straitA narrow water channel joining two seas or oceans.
  • plateauHigh land with a fairly flat top and often steep sides.
  • steppeA large, flat, unforested grassland with a temperate climate.
  • tundraA cold, treeless plain whose soil stays frozen for much of the time.
  • permafrostFrozen tundra soil that traps greenhouse gases.
  • savannahA tropical grassland with open spaces, scattered trees and seasonal rain.
  • salt flatFlat land covered with a layer of salt.
