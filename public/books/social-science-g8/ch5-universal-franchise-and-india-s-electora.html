Words this chapter explains

  • Universal adult franchiseEvery adult citizen can vote once, with every vote having equal value.
  • FranchiseA legal right to vote granted by the Constitution or government.
  • DemocracyA system in which eligible citizens take part in choosing representatives.
  • ConstituencyAn area whose voters elect a representative to a legislative body.
  • Electoral systemThe connected rules and institutions used to hold elections.
  • Secret ballotA voting method in which nobody knows who voted for whom.
  • Ballot paperA paper on which a voter marks a candidate’s name.
  • Election Commission of IndiaThe independent constitutional body that conducts free and fair elections.
  • Model Code of ConductRules for leaders, parties and candidates that help keep elections free and fair.
  • NOTAAn option that allows voters to reject all the candidates.
  • Electronic voting machineA machine used to record a voter’s electronic vote.
  • Voter Verifiable Paper Audit TrailA system that produces a paper record for checking electronic votes.
  • CoalitionTwo or more political parties working together.
  • Member of ParliamentA person elected to represent people in the national Parliament.
  • Member of Legislative AssemblyA person elected to represent people in a state assembly.
  • Electoral collegeA group formed to carry out the election of the President.
