Words this chapter explains

  • citizenSomeone who legally belongs to a country and joins its political community.
  • residentSomeone who lives in a place but may not be a citizen there.
  • rightsClaims people are entitled to so they can live with dignity, safety and respect.
  • dutiesResponsibilities that citizens must follow as members of a community.
  • fundamental rightsBasic rights that every Indian citizen has under the Constitution.
  • equalityTreating every person equally before the law and giving equal opportunities.
  • equityGiving people the support they need while taking account of existing inequalities.
  • bonded labourForcing people to work without fair wages.
  • human traffickingTrading or selling people for profit.
  • child labourMaking children work instead of attending school.
  • constitutional remediesThe right to approach the Supreme Court when fundamental rights are violated.
  • discriminationMistreating a person or group because of identity, background or appearance.
  • inclusionCreating an environment where everyone feels welcomed, respected, valued and included.
