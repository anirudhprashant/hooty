Words this chapter explains

  • DemographyIt studies the size, structure and changes of human populations.
  • Population structureIt describes a population by age, gender, occupation, education and similar characteristics.
  • Working-age populationThese are people aged 15 to 64 who are generally considered economically productive.
  • CensusIt is an official count of all people in a country at a specific time.
  • ProjectionIt is an estimate of a future situation based on present trends.
  • Birth rateIt counts live births in a year per 1,000 people.
  • Fertility rateIt is the average number of children a woman would have in her lifetime.
  • Replacement level fertility rateIt is the fertility rate of 2.1, linked to replacing parents with children.
  • Death or mortality rateIt counts deaths in a population during a year, usually per 1,000 people.
  • MigrationIt is the movement of people to another place to live temporarily or permanently.
  • Sex ratioIt shows the number of females for every 1,000 males.
  • Population pyramidIt is a graph showing people by age group and gender.
  • Youth bulgeIt is a large population of people aged 16 to 30.
  • Ageing populationIt means a population has more elderly people and fewer young people.
