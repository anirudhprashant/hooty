Words this chapter explains

  • Regenerative economyAn economy that restores and replaces used or depleted resources instead of wasting them.
  • Natural resourcesMaterials and substances that occur in Nature and are valuable to humans.
  • ExploitationIn this chapter, it means extracting, using and consuming natural resources.
  • Renewable resourcesResources that can renew or replenish themselves if people use them wisely.
  • RestorationReturning something that has been damaged to its original healthy state.
  • RegenerationNature’s ability to create new life and the conditions for thriving.
  • SustainabilityUsing resources in a way that allows them to continue without damaging Nature’s cycles.
  • Ecosystem servicesBenefits people receive from natural processes such as clean water and pollinated crops.
  • Non-renewable resourcesResources that cannot be replenished at the rate people use them.
  • Natural resource curseThe situation where plenty of natural resources does not automatically make a country rich.
  • Biodiversity lossA decline in the variety of life on Earth.
  • GroundwaterWater held below the ground that people can extract for uses such as irrigation.
  • StewardshipActing as a caretaker of natural resources so they can be restored and regenerated.
  • VṛikṣhāyurvedaAn ancient Indian science about caring for plants and trees.
  • LokasangrahaActing for the wellbeing of everyone rather than only for oneself.
  • OverexploitationUsing a resource faster than Nature can restore or regenerate it.
