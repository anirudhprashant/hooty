Words this chapter explains

  • MarathaA person belonging to the group native to the Deccan plateau.
  • Deccan plateauThe plateau where the Marathas were native, especially present-day Maharashtra.
  • bhaktiA path of devotion to the divine or a particular deity.
  • jāgīrLand granted to a noble or soldier as a reward for service.
  • SwarājyaShivaji’s vision of a kingdom governed by his own people.
  • guerrilla warfareFighting in which small, fast groups use surprise and local knowledge.
  • dakṣhiṇa-digvijayaShivaji’s campaign to conquer southern parts of India.
  • PeshwaThe Maratha office that came to hold great political influence.
  • aṣhṭa pradhāna manḍalaA council of eight ministers who assisted the ruler.
  • chauthA tax of 25 per cent levied by the Marathas.
  • sardeshmukhiAn additional tax of 10 per cent charged with chauth.
  • bārgīrsCavalry soldiers whose horses and equipment were paid for by the state.
  • shiledārsCavalry soldiers who paid for their own horses and equipment.
  • panchāyatA local gathering of officials and prominent men that delivered justice.
  • kotwālAn official deployed to maintain law and order in a city.
  • cartazA pass that European powers required Indian ships to purchase.
