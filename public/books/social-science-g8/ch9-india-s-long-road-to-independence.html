Words this chapter explains

  • autonomyFreedom to govern oneself without outside control.
  • colonial ruleControl of a country by a foreign power.
  • princely statesIndian states ruled by local princes under British supremacy.
  • paramountcyThe British power to remain the highest authority over Indian rulers.
  • reformA change intended to improve society or its institutions.
  • SwadeshiBuying and promoting products made in one’s own country.
  • boycottRefusing to buy or use something as a form of protest.
  • satyagrahaA method of protest based on truth and peaceful resistance.
  • ahimsaThe belief that violence against people is wrong.
  • separate electoratesA system in which members of one community vote separately for representatives.
  • Dominion statusSelf-government under the British Crown with some British control.
  • Purna SwarajComplete freedom from foreign rule.
  • Civil DisobedienceA movement in which people deliberately broke unjust laws.
  • untouchabilityDiscrimination against people treated as outside the caste system.
  • Azad HindThe provisional free Indian government formed by Subhas Chandra Bose.
  • PartitionThe division of British India into India and Pakistan in 1947.
