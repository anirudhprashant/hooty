Words this chapter explains

  • propositionA statement that is either true or false.
  • converseThe proposition obtained by exchanging the two parts of an if-then statement.
  • implicationA statement written as “X implies Y” instead of “if X then Y”.
  • counterexampleAn example that shows a proposition need not be true.
  • primeA number described as prime in the chapter’s number claims.
