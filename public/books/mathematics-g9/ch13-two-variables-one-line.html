  • linear equation in two variablesAn equation involving the two variables x and y.

  • solutionA pair of x- and y-values that satisfies the given equation.
  • ordered pairA pair of numbers in which the first is the x-value and the second is the y-value.
  • substitution methodA method that replaces one variable with an expression in the other.
  • elimination methodA method that removes one variable from a pair of equations.
