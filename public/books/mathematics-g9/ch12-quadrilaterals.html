Words this chapter explains

  • quadrilateralA figure made from four segments joining four distinct points.
  • tilingCovering the plane with copies of a shape without gaps or overlaps.
  • parallelogramA quadrilateral whose opposite sides are parallel.
  • vertexA corner point of a quadrilateral.
  • sideOne of the four segments joining the vertices of a quadrilateral.
  • diagonalA segment joining two opposite vertices of a quadrilateral.
  • internal angleAn angle formed at a vertex by a pair of adjacent sides.
  • convexA quadrilateral without a dent, with all internal angles below 180°.
  • non-convexA quadrilateral with a dent and an internal angle above 180°.
  • self-intersectingA quadrilateral in which two of its sides cross.
  • non-planarA quadrilateral whose four vertices are not in one plane.
  • midpoint theoremThe segment joining two side midpoints is parallel to the third side and half its length.
  • centroidThe common point where a triangle’s three medians meet.
  • Varignon parallelogramThe parallelogram formed by joining the midpoints of a quadrilateral’s four sides.
