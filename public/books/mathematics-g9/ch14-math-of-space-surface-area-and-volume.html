Words this chapter explains

  • solidA solid has an outer surface and occupies space.
  • volumeVolume shows how much three-dimensional space an object occupies.
  • netA flat pattern that can be folded and joined to make a solid.
  • curved surface areaThe area of the curved part without the flat ends.
  • right circular cylinderA cylinder whose axis is perpendicular to its circular base.
  • cross-sectionThe shape seen when a very thin slice is taken through an object.
  • coneA solid with a circular base and one pointed apex.
  • slant lengthThe cone measurement labelled l in l² = h² + r².
  • pyramidA solid with a flat base whose points join to one apex.
  • apexThe single point where the base points of a pyramid meet.
  • sphereA round solid whose surface points are the same distance from its centre.
  • hemisphereOne of two equal halves made by cutting a sphere through its centre.
  • guesstimateA sensible estimate made when exact data are unavailable.
  • unit cubeA cube with one unit in each direction, used to set cubic units.
