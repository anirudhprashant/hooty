Words this chapter explains

  • sequenceAn ordered list of numbers or other objects in a particular order.
  • finite sequenceA sequence with a stated end and a fixed number of terms.
  • infinite sequenceA sequence that continues indefinitely.
  • explicit rule/formulaA rule using n, the term's position, to calculate the term's value.
  • recursive rule/formulaA rule that relates a term to earlier terms.
  • square numberA member of the sequence 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, ….
  • natural numberOne of the numbers 1, 2, 3, … in the natural-number sequence.
  • arithmetic progressionA sequence in which the difference between consecutive terms is constant.
  • common differenceThe fixed difference between consecutive terms of an AP.
  • geometric progressionA sequence in which each term is obtained by multiplying the previous term by a fixed number.
  • common ratioThe fixed multiplier between consecutive terms of a GP.
  • fractalA shape or pattern that repeats itself at different scales.
  • Sierpiński triangleA fractal triangle formed by removing the middle triangle and repeating the process.
  • Virahānka–Fibonacci sequenceA sequence in which each term is the sum of the previous two.
