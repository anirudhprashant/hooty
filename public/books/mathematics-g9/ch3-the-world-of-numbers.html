Words this chapter explains

  • one-to-one correspondenceThis means matching each object with one other object.
  • Natural NumbersThese are the counting numbers that begin with 1.
  • parārdhaThis was the name given to 10 to the power 12.
  • ŚhūnyaThis Sanskrit word means zero.
  • ŚhūnyatāThis means a state of emptiness or nothingness.
  • binduThis is a dot used as a symbol for zero.
  • additive inverseThis is a number that cancels another when the two are added.
  • Rational NumbersThese are all numbers that can be written as ratios of integers.
  • equivalent fractionsThese fractions have the same value even if they look different.
  • co-primeThis means sharing no common factor apart from 1.
  • denseThis means another rational number can always be found between two rational numbers.
  • Irrational NumbersThese are numbers that cannot be written as ratios of integers.
  • Proof by ContradictionThis proves a statement by assuming its opposite and reaching a contradiction.
  • cyclic numberThis is a repeating block whose digits shift in a circle when multiplied.
