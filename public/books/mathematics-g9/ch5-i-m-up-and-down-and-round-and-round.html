Words this chapter explains

  • equidistantEquidistant points are at the same distance from a given point.
  • locusA locus is the set of all points satisfying a given condition.
  • radiusThe radius is the distance from the centre to a point on the circle.
  • chordA chord is a line segment with both endpoints on a circle.
  • diameterA diameter is a chord that passes through the circle’s centre.
  • rotational symmetryRotational symmetry means a shape looks unchanged after turning it.
  • reflection symmetryReflection symmetry means a shape matches its mirror image across a line.
  • perpendicular bisectorA perpendicular bisector is the line of points equally distant from two endpoints.
  • circumcircleA circumcircle passes through all three vertices of a triangle.
  • circumcentreThe circumcentre is the centre of a triangle’s circumcircle.
  • arcAn arc is a connected curved part of a circle.
  • major arcA major arc is the larger of the two arcs between two points.
  • minor arcA minor arc is the smaller of the two arcs between two points.
  • concyclicConcyclic points are points that lie on the same circle.
  • cyclic quadrilateralA cyclic quadrilateral has all four vertices on one circle.
  • corollaryA corollary is a result that follows directly from an already proved result.
