Words this chapter explains

  • grainA grain, such as rice or wheat, can form part of our diet.
  • dietA diet includes food items made from grains, pulses and vegetables.
  • ingredientsIngredients are the things used to make food items.
  • farmerFarmers are among the people whose efforts make a grain’s journey possible.
  • sellerSellers also help make a grain’s journey possible.
  • gratitudeGratitude means showing thankfulness for contributors and for the food we eat.
  • festivalSome Indian festivals show gratitude to Mother Nature for our food.
  • mindful eatingMindful eating means paying attention to what we eat and how we feel while eating.
  • flavourWe use our tongue, nose and other senses to feel flavour and taste.
  • textureWe feel the food’s texture before swallowing it.
  • waste foodWasting food disrespects the hard efforts of everyone who helped bring it to us.
  • physical activityPhysical activities include playing in open fields, running, jumping, cycling and skipping.
  • yogaYoga improves our posture and helps us stay happy and active.
  • sufficient sleepSufficient sleep means enough sleep every day.
  • safe touchA comforting hug from a family member can be a safe touch.
  • unsafe touchAn unsafe touch can make us feel uneasy, scared, confused or uncomfortable.
