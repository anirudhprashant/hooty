Words this chapter explains

  • PalashPalash is the tree with orange-red flowers that is also called the flame of forest.
  • solar-poweredIt means that the village gets its power from the sun.
  • solar panelA solar panel generates electricity for things such as fans and bulbs.
  • dyeA dye is the coloured liquid left after plant materials are removed.
  • Gond artGond art is painting on walls with natural colours.
  • natural extractsNatural extracts are colouring materials taken from flowers and leaves.
  • earthen potAn earthen pot is a pot for storing grains that is lined with neem leaves.
  • bamboo basketA bamboo basket stores grains and is coated on the outside with cow dung.
  • colouring propertiesColouring properties mean that plant parts can give colour to a dye.
  • Jenu KurubasThe Jenu Kurubas are a Karnataka tribe whose name comes from a word meaning honey.
  • beehiveA beehive is where the Jenu Kurubas collect honey.
  • first-aid boxA first-aid box holds medicines and mosquito repellent cream.
  • mosquito repellentMosquito repellent is used to keep mosquitoes from biting.
  • Sacred GroveA Sacred Grove is a small forest protected by local communities.
  • TumriA Tumri is a grain container made with a round or oval dried bottle gourd.
  • herbal gardenA herbal garden is a space for showing different herbs and their names.
