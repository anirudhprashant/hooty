Words this chapter explains

  • recycled paperThis is paper made again from used paper.
  • paper pulpPulp is soaked paper mashed into a wet mixture.
  • fenugreek seedsThese seeds help bind the mashed paper together.
  • binding materialA binding material helps hold the paper mixture together.
  • natural dyesThese are colours made from different parts of plants.
  • artificial dyesPaper can be painted with artificial dyes as well as natural ones.
  • talapatraTalapatra is the name for palm leaves used in India for writing.
  • environment-friendlyThis kind of material helps protect the environment.
  • cardboardThis is thick, hard paper used for making boxes.
  • BrailleBraille helps visually challenged people read and write by feeling tiny bumps.
  • 5RsThe 5Rs are five ways to reduce waste.
  • RefuseThis means saying no to things that make waste.
  • ReduceThis means using things carefully so that less waste is made.
  • ReuseThis means using something many times before throwing it away.
  • RepurposeThis means using something in a new and creative way.
  • RecycleThis means using steps to turn a used thing into something new.
