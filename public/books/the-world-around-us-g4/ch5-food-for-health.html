Words this chapter explains

  • shad rasaThe six tastes are sweet, sour, salty, pungent, bitter and astringent.
  • AyurvedaAyurveda calls these six tastes ‘shad rasa’.
  • milletMillet is a grain used to make foods such as roti and upma.
  • nutrientsNutrients are the parts of food that our bodies need.
  • energy-giving foodsThese foods give energy to our body.
  • body-building foodsThese foods help us grow, build muscles and become strong.
  • protective foodsThese foods help protect us from diseases.
  • food groupsThe three groups are energy-giving, body-building and protective foods.
  • junk foodThese are foods high in oil, salt or sugar that are not healthy when eaten in large quantities.
  • balanced plateA plate with the right quantities of foods from all three groups.
  • thaliA regional meal with different foods, tastes and health benefits.
  • digestWater helps the body digest food.
  • food diaryA record of what you eat at each meal for a week.
  • collageA picture made by sticking cut-out food pictures onto paper.
  • chefA professional cook who is skilled in preparing food.
  • solar cookerA cooker that uses sunlight instead of fire or electricity.
