Words this chapter explains

  • shadowA dark shape that appears when an object stops light from passing.
  • sunlightLight that comes from the Sun.
  • source of lightThe thing that shines light on an object.
  • sundialA device that tells time using a shadow.
  • Jantar MantarA place built by Raja Jai Singh that has a sundial.
  • Samrat YantraA sundial that can tell time accurately from its shadow.
  • shadow puppetryAn activity using shadows that has been practised in India for a long time.
  • night skyThe sky we see after sunset, filled with stars.
  • twinkling starsStars that shine with a flickering look in the night sky.
  • PoornimaThe name for the complete circle shape of the Moon.
  • AmavasyaThe name for the new Moon, when the Moon cannot be seen.
