Words this chapter explains

  • conservationConservation means using water carefully and not wasting it.
  • storage structuresStorage structures are large places built to hold water.
  • overhead water tanksOverhead water tanks are tanks kept or built on roofs.
  • uniqueUnique means special or different from others.
  • steel rodsSteel rods make the frame of a water tank.
  • concreteConcrete is used to give a tank its final shape.
  • contractionsContractions are short forms made by joining two words.
  • apostropheAn apostrophe takes the place of letters left out.
  • exclamatory sentencesExclamatory sentences show strong feelings.
  • emotionsEmotions are feelings such as amazement and wonder.
  • wastageWastage means using more water than we need.
  • neighbourhoodA neighbourhood is the area around people’s homes.
  • preciousPrecious means very valuable and important.
  • rainwater harvestingRainwater harvesting is a system for collecting rainwater to use.
  • slogansSlogans are short messages that encourage people.
  • poster-makingPoster-making means making posters for a message.
