  • gilliThe gilli is the small peg that is hit into the air.
  • dandaThe danda is the long stick used to hit the gilli.
  • prefixA prefix is added to the beginning of a word to change its meaning.
  • un-The prefix un- means not.
  • re-The prefix re- means again.
  • abstract nounAn abstract noun names a feeling, thought or idea without a physical shape.
  • physical shapeA physical shape is the form that can be seen or touched.
  • possessive pronounA possessive pronoun shows ownership or a relationship.
  • ownershipOwnership shows that something belongs to someone.
  • mineMine means belonging to the speaker.

  • gatherGather means to come together in one place.
  • quitQuit means to stop doing an activity.
