Words this chapter explains

  • congruentTwo figures are congruent when they have the same shape and size.
  • superimposeTo superimpose is to place one figure exactly over another.
  • replicaA replica is an exact copy.
  • sidelengthsSidelengths are the lengths of a figure's sides.
  • SSSSSS is enough to guarantee congruence when all three sides are equal.
  • SASSAS uses two equal sides and their equal included angle.
  • SSASSA uses two sides and a non-included angle, so it does not always guarantee congruence.
  • ASAASA uses two equal angles and their included side.
  • AASAAS uses two equal angles and a non-included side.
  • RHSRHS uses a right angle, a hypotenuse and another equal side.
  • hypotenuseThe hypotenuse is the side opposite the right angle.
  • isoscelesThe chapter's isosceles triangle has equal sides AB and AC.
  • equilateralAn equilateral triangle has three equal sides and three equal angles.
  • corresponding verticesCorresponding vertices are matching vertices in congruent triangles.
  • midpointA midpoint divides a line segment into two equal parts.
