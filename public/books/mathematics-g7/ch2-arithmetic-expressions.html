Words this chapter explains

  • arithmetic expressionA mathematical phrase made from numbers and operation signs.
  • valueThe value of an expression is the number it evaluates to.
  • equality signThe equality sign shows that two things have the same value.
  • operationAn operation is a sign used to combine numbers in an expression.
  • sumA sum is what is found when numbers are added.
  • productA product is what is found when numbers are multiplied.
  • comparison signA comparison sign shows whether two values are equal, greater or less.
  • bracketBrackets show which part of an expression must be evaluated first.
  • termA term is a part of an expression separated by plus signs.
  • inverseThe inverse of a number has the opposite sign.
  • evaluateEvaluating means finding the value of an expression.
  • commutative propertyThis property says that swapping terms does not change the sum.
  • associative propertyThis property says that grouping terms does not change the sum.
  • distributive propertyThis property says that multiplying a sum by a number equals multiplying each term by it.
