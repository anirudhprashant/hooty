Words this chapter explains

  • plane surfaceA flat surface such as a table top or a sheet of paper.
  • intersecting linesTwo lines that meet each other at a point.
  • angleA shape formed where two lines meet.
  • straight angleAn angle that measures 180 degrees.
  • adjacent anglesTwo angles that lie next to each other.
  • linear pairTwo adjacent angles that always add up to 180 degrees.
  • vertically opposite anglesA pair of opposite angles that are equal to each other.
  • proofA justification that shows why a mathematical statement is true.
  • perpendicular linesTwo lines that meet at right angles of 90 degrees.
  • parallel linesTwo lines on the same plane that never meet.
  • line segmentA straight part of a line with two endpoints.
  • transversalA line that intersects two other lines.
  • corresponding anglesAngles in the same relative position where a transversal meets two lines.
  • alternate anglesAngles on opposite sides of a transversal between two lines.
  • interior anglesAngles lying between two lines crossed by a transversal.
