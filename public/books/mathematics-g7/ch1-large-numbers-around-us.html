Words this chapter explains

  • lakhA lakh is one hundred thousand, written as 1,00,000.
  • croreA crore is one hundred lakhs, written as 1,00,00,000.
  • arabAn arab is one hundred crores, written as 1,000,000,000.
  • Indian place value systemThis system groups digits from the right in a 3-2-2-2 pattern.
  • American systemThis system groups digits from the right in a 3-3-3-3 pattern.
  • number nameA number name shows how a number is read in words.
  • place value notationThis notation places commas according to the Indian number system.
  • approximationAn approximation is a number that is close to the exact value.
  • rounding upRounding up gives an approximated number greater than the actual number.
  • rounding downRounding down gives an approximated number smaller than the actual number.
  • nearest neighboursThese are the rounded numbers closest to a given number.
  • indigenous varietiesThese are local rice varieties collected from different places.
  • seed bankA seed bank collects and stores seeds of different varieties.
  • multiplication shortcutThis method rearranges a multiplication to make it quicker.
  • regroupingRegrouping rearranges factors so that multiplication becomes easier.
  • ten thousands placeThis is the place of a digit worth ten thousand.
