Words this chapter explains

  • statistical statementA claim or summary about something that uses numbers, proportions, probabilities or predictions.
  • statistical questionA question that can be answered by collecting and studying data.
  • statisticsThe study of collecting, organising, analysing, interpreting and presenting data.
  • phenomenonSomething that happens or that we can observe and study.
  • averageA representative value found by dividing the total of all values by their number.
  • representative valueOne number that gives useful information about a group of numbers.
  • fair-shareThe equal share that each person would receive if a group’s total were divided equally.
  • variabilityThe way values in data are spread out or clustered together.
  • dot plotA graph that shows data values as dots placed along a line.
  • medianThe middle value in sorted data, or the mean of the two middle values when there are an even number of values.
  • outlierA value that differs greatly from most other values in the data.
  • central tendencyThe tendency of data values to gather around a centre represented by the mean or median.
  • data visualisationShowing data in a picture or graph so that it is easier to understand.
  • clustered column graphA graph that places bars from different groups side by side for comparison.
  • double column graphA clustered graph with two bars in each group.
