Words this chapter explains

  • AtmosphereThe layer of gases surrounding the Earth and supporting life.
  • GravityA force that pulls objects with mass or energy towards one another.
  • AltitudeThe height of a place measured above mean sea level.
  • TropopauseThe boundary between the troposphere and the stratosphere.
  • StratopauseThe boundary between the stratosphere and the mesosphere.
  • IonosphereA part of the thermosphere that helps radio waves return towards Earth.
  • AuroraA colourful display of light seen near the polar skies.
  • InsolationThe Sun’s energy that reaches and is intercepted by the Earth.
  • HumidityThe presence of water vapour in the air.
  • Atmospheric pressureThe pressure exerted by the weight of air on the Earth’s surface.
  • NakshatrasThe 27 fixed sections of the sky through which the Moon passes.
  • Greenhouse gasesGases that trap heat in the atmosphere.
  • Carbon footprintThe total greenhouse gases released through human activities.
  • Dhūsī bāndhA raised bank built beside a river to hold back its water.
