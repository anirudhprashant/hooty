Words this chapter explains

  • plate tectonicsThe theory that explains how Earth’s crust moves in large pieces.
  • tectonic platesLarge and small pieces of Earth’s crust that move slowly over the mantle.
  • lithosphereThe rigid outer layer made of the crust and upper mantle.
  • asthenosphereA hot, semi-molten layer that allows tectonic plates to move.
  • convection currentsCircular movements of mantle material that push and pull tectonic plates.
  • plate boundariesThe edges where tectonic plates meet each other.
  • weatheringThe breaking down of surface rocks without moving the broken pieces.
  • erosionThe wearing away and movement of soil, rocks and other surface materials.
  • agents of gradationNatural forces that wear down, move and deposit materials on the surface.
  • deltaA fan-shaped or triangular area formed where a river deposits sediments.
  • morainesLandforms made of material deposited by melting glaciers.
  • Karst topographyUnderground landforms created by water dissolving soluble rocks.
  • landslidesSudden failures of unstable slopes caused by natural and human factors.
