Words this chapter explains

  • biomeA biome is a large natural region with particular plants, animals and climate.
  • ecosystemAn ecosystem is a place where living and non-living things interact closely.
  • terrestrial biomeA terrestrial biome is a land-based region such as a forest or desert.
  • aquatic biomeAn aquatic biome is a water-based region such as a lake or ocean.
  • permafrostPermafrost is soil that stays frozen almost throughout the year.
  • producerA producer is a plant or alga that captures solar energy.
  • trophic levelA trophic level is one level of organisms within a biome pyramid.
  • biodiversityBiodiversity is the variety of life, habitats and genetic differences on Earth.
  • biodiversity hotspotA biodiversity hotspot is a region with unique species found nowhere else.
  • wildlife corridorA wildlife corridor is a route that allows animals to move between habitats.
  • ecotourismEcotourism is responsible travel that supports nature and local communities.
  • non-timber forest productA non-timber forest product is a useful forest product other than timber.
  • sacred groveA sacred grove is a community-protected forest patch with religious importance.
  • Geographical IndicationA Geographical Inditation identifies a product linked to a particular region.
  • Forest Rights ActThe Forest Rights Act recognises eligible communities' traditional rights over forest resources.
  • ecological commonsEcological commons are natural resources shared and used by a whole community.
