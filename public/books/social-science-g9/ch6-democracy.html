Words this chapter explains

  • democracyA system of self-governance in which people take part in decision-making.
  • popular sovereigntyThe principle that the people hold the ultimate source of political power.
  • rule of lawThe principle that everyone is equal before the law and no one is above it.
  • Fundamental RightsConstitutional rights that protect citizens’ freedom, dignity and equality.
  • separation of powersThe division of government responsibilities among the legislature, executive and judiciary.
  • accountabilityThe duty of democratic institutions to answer to citizens for their actions.
  • transparencyOpenness about government information, decisions and actions.
  • multi-party systemA system where several parties contest elections and represent different interests.
  • coalitionAn alliance of parties that together seek to form government.
  • oppositionThe largest group of elected representatives opposing the government.
  • federalismA system that shares powers between the union and state governments.
  • civic responsibilityRespectful and lawful behaviour that helps maintain democratic life.
