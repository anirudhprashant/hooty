Words this chapter explains

  • janaA jana was a group of people joined by kinship ties.
  • sabhāA sabhā was a smaller assembly that mainly handled judicial work.
  • janapadaA janapada was a political unit based on a shared territory.
  • mahājanapadaA mahājanapada was a larger political unit than a janapada.
  • dhammaDhamma was the Pāli term used for ethical principles similar to dharma.
  • samatvaSamatva was the idea that all beings share the same basic reality.
  • jātiA jāti was a distinct social group formed within wider society.
  • āśhramaAn āśhrama was one of four stages through which a person’s life passed.
  • puruṣārthaA puruṣārtha was one of the four main goals of life.
  • BhaktiBhakti was a path of devotion offering a direct connection with the gods.
  • gurukulaA gurukula was the teacher’s home where pupils lived and studied.
  • ballot potThe ballot pot was a Chola system for choosing village representatives by chance.
