Words this chapter explains

  • Submarine reliefThese are the physical landforms found on the ocean floor.
  • Continental shelfThis is the shallow underwater edge of a continent.
  • Continental slopeThis steep slope marks the true edge of a continent.
  • Abyssal plainThis is a vast, flat and deep part of the ocean floor.
  • Mid-Ocean RidgeThis is a huge underwater mountain range formed by volcanic activity.
  • WavelengthThis is the distance between two consecutive wave crests or troughs.
  • Run-up heightThis is the greatest height that a tsunami reaches above sea level.
  • Spring tideThis tide occurs when the Sun, Earth and Moon line up.
  • Neap tideThis tide occurs when the Sun and Moon pull in different directions.
  • Coriolis forceEarth's rotation bends the path of freely moving objects.
  • BathymetryThis is the study and mapping of underwater landforms.
  • Blue EconomyThis means using ocean resources sustainably for growth and better livelihoods.
  • Exclusive Economic ZoneThis is an ocean area where a country has special rights to use resources.
  • UNCLOSThis international convention defines maritime zones.
  • COLREGThese international rules help ships avoid collisions.
  • MARPOLThis international agreement aims to reduce pollution caused by ships.
