Words this chapter explains

  • Social ScienceThis subject systematically studies human society and its connections.
  • PañchamahābhūtasThis idea describes nature as a system of five fundamental elements.
  • vasudhaiva kuṭumbakamThis expression means that all people belong to one worldwide family.
  • ArthaśhāstraThis ancient work examines administration, the economy, taxation, and rulers’ duties.
  • empirical evidenceThis means information gained through actual observation or experimentation.
  • genealogical recordsThese records trace family relationships and important events across generations.
  • archaeological sourcesThese are physical remains used to study the past.
  • epigraphic sourceThis is a historical source made from inscriptions on lasting materials.
  • numismatic sourceThis is a historical source that uses coins, currency, or medals.
  • holistic approachThis means studying a subject as a complete and connected whole.
  • Panchayati RajThis system supports local self-governance and public participation in villages.
  • grassroots democracyThis means citizens take part in decisions and planning at the local level.
  • dharmaThis means the moral duties a person should follow.
  • arthaThis refers to a ruler’s responsibility for the economic welfare of society.
  • rājadharmaThis means the duties and responsibilities of a ruler.
  • GeographyThis discipline studies places, environments, and human connections with their surroundings.
