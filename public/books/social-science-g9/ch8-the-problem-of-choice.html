Words this chapter explains

  • marketA place where products and services are bought and sold.
  • needsImportant requirements that people must satisfy, such as food, water and shelter.
  • wantsThings that people would like to have but do not always need.
  • resourcesFactors used to produce goods and services.
  • opportunity costThe value of the option given up when another option is chosen.
  • Production Possibility CurveA curve showing different combinations of goods that can be produced with available resources.
  • factors of productionLand, labour, capital and technology used to produce goods and services.
  • economic entityA person or group that takes part in an economic activity.
  • dataFacts and statistics collected together for reference or analysis.
  • surveysA systematic way of collecting and analysing information about economic conditions and behaviour.
  • scarcityThe shortage of resources in relation to unlimited wants.
  • economic systemThe system used to organise production, consumption and distribution in a country.
  • planned economyAn economic system in which the government controls resources and prices.
  • market economyAn economic system in which market forces mainly determine resource allocation and prices.
  • mixed economyAn economy in which the government and private sector both take part in the market.
