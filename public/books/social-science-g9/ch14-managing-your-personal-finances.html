Words this chapter explains

  • Personal financePlanning and managing your own money for needs and wants.
  • IncomeMoney received regularly or sometimes for products or services.
  • BudgetA written plan that balances income with expenses.
  • SavingKeeping some income aside for future use.
  • LiquidityHow quickly and easily an asset can become cash.
  • InflationA rise in prices that reduces what money can buy.
  • CompoundingReinvesting earnings so that they can earn more over time.
  • Fixed depositMoney placed with a bank or post office for a fixed period.
  • BondAn investment through which you lend money for a fixed period.
  • Maturity periodThe time between a bond’s issue and the repayment of its principal.
  • Portfolio diversificationSpreading investments across different types of assets.
  • InsuranceA contract that provides financial compensation for covered losses.
  • PremiumThe regular amount paid to an insurance company.
  • EMIA fixed monthly amount used to repay a loan.
  • Tax slabA range of taxable income with a specified tax rate.
