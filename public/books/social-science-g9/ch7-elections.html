Words this chapter explains

  • direct electionAn election in which citizens vote directly for their representatives or leaders.
  • indirect electionAn election in which citizens choose representatives who then elect the leaders.
  • representationBeing represented by people who make decisions on one’s behalf.
  • psephologyThe scientific study of elections.
  • constituencyA geographical area used for electing representatives to public offices.
  • FPTPA system in which the candidate receiving the most votes wins.
  • proportional representationA system that gives parties seats according to their share of votes.
  • quotaThe minimum number of votes needed to win an election.
  • delimitationThe process of deciding the number of seats and fixing constituency boundaries.
  • electoral rollThe official list of people registered to vote in a constituency.
  • Special Intensive RevisionAn exercise that updates, verifies and corrects electoral rolls.
  • Model Code of ConductThe rules that guide fair behaviour during an election.
  • political partyAn organisation that presents policies and contests elections.
  • defectionLeaving or abandoning the party on whose ticket a person was elected.
  • Electronically Transmitted Postal Ballot SystemA system that lets service voters cast postal ballots electronically.
