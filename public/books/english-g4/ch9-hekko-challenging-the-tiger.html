Words this chapter explains

  • HekHek means to challenge.
  • koKo means tiger.
  • HekkoHekko is the name of the challenge against the tiger.
  • AamiAami is the name of the Man team.
  • AakhoAakho is the name of the Tiger team.
  • superiorityThe story says that humans and animals disagreed about who was better.
  • disagreementA disagreement happens when people or groups do not agree.
  • tossA toss before the game decides which team is called Aami or Aakho.
  • human chainThe Aami players make a human chain to stay inside the circle.
  • JokoJoko means surrender.
  • pointsA team gets points when it puts an opponent out.
  • KhozubaKhozuba is the title given to the fastest Aami player.
  • KhotibaKhotiba is the title given to the strongest Aakho player.
  • decisionDecide and decision are related words for making a choice.
  • past simple tensePast simple tense shows that events happened some time ago.
  • irregular verbsIrregular verbs do not add -ed when changed to the past form.
