Words this chapter explains

  • traffic policeTraffic police help people follow road rules and stay safe.
  • trafficTraffic means all the vehicles moving along roads.
  • safetySafety means being protected from harm on the roads.
  • zebra crossingA zebra crossing is a black and white strip where people can cross a road.
  • traffic lightsTraffic lights control movement on very busy roads.
  • pedestrian signalA pedestrian signal shows people when it is time to cross.
  • footpathA footpath is a place made for pedestrians to walk.
  • pedestrianA pedestrian is a person on foot, such as someone using a footpath.
  • reflectiveReflective things help drivers see a person clearly in the evening.
  • distractTo distract someone means to take their attention away from something important.
  • naming wordA naming word is a word that names a person, place, thing, or idea.
  • countable nounA countable noun names things that can be counted.
  • uncountable nounAn uncountable noun names something that cannot be counted individually.
  • accident-freeAccident-free means having no accidents.
