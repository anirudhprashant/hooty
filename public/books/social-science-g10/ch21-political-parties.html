Words this chapter explains

  • democracyA political system in which parties contest elections and help in running government.
  • political partyA group that comes together to contest elections and hold power in government.
  • policies and programmesThe plans and actions that a party promises to follow for society.
  • collective goodThe benefit that is shared by people as a group or society.
  • partisanA person who strongly takes one side and cannot view an issue in a balanced way.
  • constituencyThe local area for which an elected representative is responsible.
  • oppositionA party that lost the election and criticises the party in power.
  • one-party systemA system in which only one party is allowed to control the government.
  • two-party systemA system in which two main parties have serious chances of forming government.
  • multiparty systemA system in which several parties compete and may join together to form government.
  • coalitionA group of parties that comes together to form a government.
  • alliance or frontParties that join together to contest elections and try to win power.
  • recognised political partyA registered party given special status by the Election Commission.
  • defectionChanging party allegiance after being elected to a legislative body.
  • affidavitA signed statement in which a candidate gives information about property and criminal cases.
