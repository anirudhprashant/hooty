Words this chapter explains

  • economic activityWork that produces goods or services for the economy.
  • sectorA group of economic activities classified according to a chosen criterion.
  • tertiary sectorThe sector that provides services supporting production and trade.
  • final goodsGoods and services that reach consumers and are counted in total production.
  • intermediate goodsGoods used up in making final goods and services.
  • GDPThe total value of final goods and services produced in a country during one year.
  • GVAThe contribution of economic sectors after adjusting for taxes and subsidies.
  • underemploymentA situation where people appear to work but do not use their full potential.
  • disguised unemploymentHidden underemployment in which everyone has some work but no one is fully employed.
  • organised sectorWorkplaces with regular terms, government registration and legal benefits for workers.
  • unorganised sectorSmall and scattered workplaces with irregular pay and few employment protections.
  • public sectorActivities in which the government owns most assets and provides services.
