Words this chapter explains

  • calligraphyThis is the art of beautiful and stylised writing.
  • accordion bookThis was a Chinese book folded and stitched at the side.
  • ukiyoThis was an art form showing ordinary human experiences, especially urban ones.
  • vellumThis was a parchment made from the skin of animals.
  • movable typeThis machine used metal letters that could be arranged to form words.
  • printing pressThis machine applied pressure to paper to print books.
  • platenThis board was pressed onto the back of paper to make an impression from type.
  • compositorThis person arranged the text for printing.
  • galleyThis was a frame where types were laid and text was composed.
  • oral cultureThis was a culture in which knowledge and stories were passed on orally.
  • Protestant ReformationThis was a sixteenth-century movement to reform the Roman Catholic Church.
  • inquisitionThis was a Roman Catholic court for identifying and punishing heretics.
  • hereticalThese were beliefs that did not follow accepted teachings of the Church.
  • chapbookThis was a small book sold by travelling pedlars.
  • despotismThis was a system in which one person held absolute power without legal checks.
  • Vernacular Press ActThis law gave the government broad powers to censor reports and editorials.
