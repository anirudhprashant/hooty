Words this chapter explains

  • developmentDevelopment means thinking about needs and working towards a better life for everyone
  • developmental goalsA developmental goal is what a person or group hopes development will provide
  • incomeIncome is money or material goods that a person can obtain and use
  • per capita incomePer capita income is a country's total income divided by its population
  • equitable distributionEquitable distribution means that income and resources are shared more fairly among people
  • Infant Mortality RateThis shows how many children die before age one in proportion to 1,000 live births
  • Literacy RateThis is the share of people in the seven-and-above age group who are literate
  • Net Attendance RatioThis is the percentage of children aged 15–17 years who attend school
  • Public Distribution SystemThis public system supplies essential goods through ration shops
  • Body Mass IndexBMI is a person's weight in kilograms divided by the square of height in metres
  • Human Development IndexThis development measure includes education, health, income and human well-being
  • Life ExpectancyThis is the average expected length of a person's life at the time of birth
  • Purchasing Power ParityPPP makes income from different countries comparable by showing what the money can buy
  • renewable resourcesThese resources are restored by nature, although they can still be overused
  • non-renewable resourcesThese resources have a fixed stock and will eventually be exhausted
