Words this chapter explains

  • mineralA naturally occurring substance with a definite internal structure.
  • oreAn accumulation of a mineral mixed with other elements.
  • veinA smaller mineral occurrence found in a crack or cavity in a rock.
  • lodeA larger mineral occurrence found in a crack or cavity in a rock.
  • sedimentary rocksRocks in which minerals occur in beds or layers.
  • placer depositsMineral deposits found in the sands of valley floors and hill bases.
  • ferrous mineralsThe mineral group that provides a strong base for metallurgical industries.
  • magnetiteThe finest iron ore, containing up to 70 per cent iron.
  • hematiteThe most important industrial iron ore by the quantity used.
  • bauxiteA clay-like ore from which alumina and later aluminium are obtained.
  • limestoneA rock mineral used for cement making and for smelting iron ore.
  • fossil fuelA fuel such as coal that formed from ancient organic material.
  • non-conventional energyEnergy sources such as solar, wind, tidal, geothermal, biogas and atomic energy.
