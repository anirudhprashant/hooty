Words this chapter explains

  • medium of exchangeSomething used to make it easier to exchange goods and services.
  • double coincidence of wantsAn exchange occurs when each person wants what the other person is offering.
  • barter systemGoods are exchanged directly without using money.
  • currencyCurrency is the paper notes and coins used as modern money.
  • demand depositsMoney kept in a bank account that can be withdrawn on demand.
  • chequeA paper instruction asking a bank to pay a stated amount.
  • interest rateThe amount charged for borrowing money or paid for keeping deposits.
  • loanMoney or goods given now in return for payment later.
  • creditAn agreement where a borrower receives money, goods or services for future payment.
  • collateralAn asset owned by a borrower and used to guarantee repayment.
  • principalThe original amount of a loan that must be repaid.
  • terms of creditThe conditions of a loan, including interest, collateral and repayment.
  • debt-trapA situation in which debt becomes too difficult to escape.
  • Self Help GroupsSmall groups of poor people, especially women, who save and lend together.
