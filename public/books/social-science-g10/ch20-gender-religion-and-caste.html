Words this chapter explains

  • gender divisionUnequal roles and expectations that society creates for men and women.
  • sexual division of labourHousework is treated as the responsibility of women rather than men.
  • feministA person who supports equal rights and opportunities for women and men.
  • patriarchyA system that values men more and gives them power over women.
  • communal politicsPolitics that treats religion as the main basis of social community.
  • communalismUsing one religion or caste group to dominate others in political life.
  • secular stateA state with no official religion that treats religious communities equally.
  • family lawsLaws dealing with matters such as marriage, divorce, adoption and inheritance.
  • caste systemA hereditary system that placed people in castes linked to occupation and ritual.
  • caste hierarchyA ladder-like ranking of caste groups from the highest to the lowest.
  • urbanisationThe movement of people from rural areas to urban areas.
  • occupational mobilityA shift from one occupation to another, often across generations.
  • Scheduled CastesCaste groups formerly treated as outcastes and subjected to exclusion and untouchability.
  • Scheduled TribesTribes that traditionally lived secluded lives in hills and forests.
  • Other Backward ClassesA broad group of castes that the Census does not separately count.
  • universal adult franchiseThe right of every adult citizen to vote in elections.
