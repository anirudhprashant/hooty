Words this chapter explains

  • manufacturingMaking goods in large quantities by processing raw materials into more valuable products.
  • secondary sectorThe part of the economy where primary materials are made into finished goods.
  • agro-basedIndustries that obtain their raw materials from agriculture.
  • mineral-basedIndustries that use minerals and metals as raw materials.
  • basic industriesIndustries that supply materials for making other goods.
  • consumer industriesIndustries that produce goods for direct use by consumers.
  • small scale industryAn industry judged by the maximum investment allowed on a unit's assets.
  • joint sectorAn industry jointly owned and run by the state and private owners.
  • cooperative sectorAn industry owned and operated by its producers, suppliers or workers.
  • heavy industriesIndustries that use heavy raw materials and produce heavy, bulky goods.
  • light industriesIndustries that use light raw materials and produce lighter goods.
  • value chainThe complete journey from raw material to the most valuable finished product.
  • cottage industryA small industry where people work at home rather than in a large factory.
  • foreign exchangeMoney earned by selling goods and services to other countries.
  • effluentLiquid waste discharged by an industry.
