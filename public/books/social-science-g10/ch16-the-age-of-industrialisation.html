Words this chapter explains

  • industrialisationThe growth of factories and machine production that changed work and society.
  • proto-industrialisationProduction for markets before factories became the main form of industry.
  • artisanA skilled worker who makes goods by hand.
  • merchantA person who buys and sells goods, often over long distances.
  • trade guildsAssociations that trained workers, controlled production and restricted new entrants.
  • staplerA person who sorts wool according to its fibre.
  • fullerA person who gathers or thickens cloth by pleating it.
  • cardingPreparing cotton or wool fibres before they are spun.
  • cotton millA factory where cotton production processes are brought together under one roof.
  • hand labourWork done by human hands rather than by machines.
  • Spinning JennyA machine that spun several threads together by turning one wheel.
  • sepoyAn Indian soldier serving the British.
  • jobberA recruiter who found workers and gained authority over them.
  • fly shuttleA device that places weft threads into warp threads and allows wider weaving.
  • swadeshiA nationalist call to support Indian-made goods instead of foreign goods.
