Words this chapter explains

  • consumerA person who buys and uses goods and services.
  • marketplaceThe market where consumers buy goods and services.
  • rules and regulationsRequired directions that protect people and guide their behaviour.
  • exploitationUnfair treatment that takes advantage of people.
  • unfair trade practiceA way of trading that cheats or harms consumers.
  • consumer movementThe organised effort to protect consumers and promote their interests.
  • COPRAIndia’s law for protecting consumers and settling consumer disputes.
  • right to safetyThe right to protection from hazardous goods and services.
  • right to informationThe right to know about the goods, services and government work you ask about.
  • MRPThe highest printed price shown on a product.
  • RTI ActA law giving people information about government departments’ work.
  • right to chooseThe right to choose whether to keep receiving a service.
  • redressalA way of settling a consumer complaint and correcting the wrong.
  • quasijudicial machineryThe three-level system of consumer commissions.
  • mediatorA neutral person who helps settle disputes outside consumer commissions.
