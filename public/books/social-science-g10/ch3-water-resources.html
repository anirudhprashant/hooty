Words this chapter explains

  • hydrological cycleThe endless movement and reuse of water on Earth.
  • freshwaterWater that is not salty and can be used by people and plants.
  • surface run offWater flowing over the land’s surface.
  • precipitationWater that falls from clouds as rain, snow or other forms.
  • over-exploitationTaking more water than nature can safely replace.
  • drought proneLikely to suffer from serious water shortages during a drought.
  • potableSafe enough for people to drink.
  • hydraulic structuresStructures made to control and use water.
  • reservoirA large body of water stored behind a dam or barrier.
  • spillwayA section of a dam designed for water to flow over or through it.
  • multi-purpose projectsProjects that combine several uses of water, such as irrigation and electricity.
  • salinisationThe build-up of salt in soil.
  • rainwater harvestingCollecting and storing rainwater for later use.
  • tankaAn underground tank used to store drinking water.
  • khadinA traditional storage structure that helps dryland farming in Rajasthan.
