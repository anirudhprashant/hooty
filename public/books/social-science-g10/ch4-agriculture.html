Words this chapter explains

  • agricultureThe economic activity of growing crops and producing food and raw materials.
  • primary activityAn activity that directly produces basic goods such as food.
  • subsistence farmingFarming mainly carried out to meet the food needs of the farmer’s family.
  • commercial farmingFarming that uses modern inputs and grows crops mainly for sale in markets.
  • plantationFarming in which one crop is grown on a large area for industry or the market.
  • Intensive Subsistence FarmingFarming on limited land that uses heavy inputs and labour to obtain higher production.
  • slash and burnFarming in which vegetation is cleared and burned before crops are grown on the land.
  • high yielding varietySeeds developed to give a higher crop yield than ordinary seeds.
  • rabi seasonThe winter cropping season, when crops are sown from October to December.
  • kharif seasonThe cropping season that begins with the monsoon.
  • zaid seasonThe short summer season between the rabi and kharif seasons.
  • staple food cropA main food crop that is eaten by most people in a country.
  • sericultureRearing silkworms to produce silk fibre.
  • BhoodanThe donation of land to landless villagers as part of Vinoba Bhave’s movement.
  • GramdanThe donation of land by zamindars or landowners to landless people.
