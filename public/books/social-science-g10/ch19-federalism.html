Words this chapter explains

  • FederalismA system in which power is divided between a central authority and constituent units.
  • Unitary systemA system in which local or provincial units remain subordinate to the central government.
  • JurisdictionThe area over which someone has legal authority.
  • Union ListThe list of subjects of national importance handled by the Union Government.
  • State ListThe list of subjects of state and local importance handled by State Governments.
  • Residuary subjectsSubjects outside the three lists on which the Union Government may legislate.
  • Union TerritoriesAreas that do not have the powers of a State.
  • Linguistic StatesStates created on the basis of the language spoken by their people.
  • DecentralisationGiving power from central and state governments to local government.
  • Panchayati rajThe local government system in villages.
  • Gram panchayatA village council made up of several elected ward members.
  • Gram sabhaThe village meeting made up of all the village's voters.
  • Panchayat samitiA representative body formed by grouping several gram panchayats.
  • Zilla parishadThe district-level body formed by the district's panchayat samitis.
