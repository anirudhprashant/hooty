Words this chapter explains

  • ResourceAnything in our environment that can satisfy our needs if it is accessible, feasible and acceptable.
  • Sustainable developmentDevelopment that does not damage the environment and does not compromise future generations' needs.
  • Agenda 21A 1992 declaration by world leaders at Rio to achieve global sustainable development.
  • Resource planningA strategy for judicious use of resources, involving identification, planning structure and matching with national plans.
  • Land degradationDamage to land caused by continuous use without conservation and management.
  • Soil erosionThe removal of soil cover and its washing down by natural forces or human activities.
  • Alluvial soilFertile soil deposited by Himalayan rivers, found in northern plains and deltas.
  • Black soilBlack clayey soil ideal for cotton, typical of the Deccan trap region.
  • Laterite soilSoil formed under tropical and subtropical climate with alternate wet and dry season.
  • Arid soilsRed to brown sandy and saline soils of dry areas.
  • Forest soilsSoils found in hilly and mountainous areas with sufficient rain forests.
  • Contour ploughingPloughing along contour lines to slow water flow down slopes.
  • Terrace cultivationCutting steps on slopes to restrict soil erosion.
  • Strip croppingLeaving strips of grass between crops to break the force of wind.
  • Shelter beltsRows of trees planted to stabilise sand dunes and desert.
  • Net sown areaThe physical extent of land on which crops are sown and harvested.
