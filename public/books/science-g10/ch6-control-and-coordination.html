Words this chapter explains

  • control and coordinationThe way living organisms make the right action happen at the right time.
  • receptorsSpecialised tips of nerve cells that detect information from the environment.
  • neuronA nerve cell that carries information as electrical impulses.
  • dendriteThe part of a neuron where information is acquired.
  • axonThe long part of a neuron through which an impulse travels.
  • reflex arcA simple nerve connection that allows a quick response without thinking.
  • spinal cordA bundle of nerves that helps form reflex arcs and carries signals.
  • central nervous systemThe brain and spinal cord, which receive and integrate information.
  • peripheral nervous systemNerves that connect the central nervous system with other body parts.
  • cerebellumThe hind-brain part that controls precision, posture and balance.
  • medullaThe hind-brain part that controls involuntary actions like blood pressure and vomiting.
  • hormoneA chemical messenger used for control and coordination.
  • auxinA plant hormone that helps cells grow longer at the shoot tip.
  • adrenalineAn animal hormone that prepares the body for fight or running away.
  • insulinA hormone from the pancreas that regulates blood sugar.
