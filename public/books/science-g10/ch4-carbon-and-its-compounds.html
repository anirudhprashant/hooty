Words this chapter explains

  • covalent bondA bond formed when two atoms share a pair of electrons.
  • valence electronsElectrons in the outermost shell of an atom.
  • noble gas configurationA completely filled outermost shell like a noble gas.
  • tetravalentHaving a valency of four.
  • catenationThe ability of carbon atoms to bond with other carbon atoms.
  • saturated compoundsCarbon compounds with only single bonds between carbon atoms.
  • unsaturated compoundsCarbon compounds with double or triple bonds between carbon atoms.
  • structural isomersCompounds with the same molecular formula but different structures.
  • hydrocarbonsCompounds made only of carbon and hydrogen.
  • homologous seriesA series where the same functional group substitutes hydrogen in carbon chains of different lengths.
  • oxidising agentsSubstances that add oxygen to other substances.
  • esterificationReaction of an acid and an alcohol to form an ester.
  • saponificationReaction where an ester with alkali gives alcohol and soap.
  • micellesClusters of soap molecules with tails inside and ionic ends outside.
  • detergentCleansing agent that works in hard water.
