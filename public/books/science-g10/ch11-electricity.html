Words this chapter explains

  • electric currentThe flow of electric charge through a conductor.
  • electric circuitA continuous closed path through which electric current flows.
  • ampereThe SI unit of electric current; one coulomb per second.
  • coulombThe SI unit of electric charge.
  • potential differenceWork done to move a unit charge between two points.
  • voltThe SI unit of potential difference.
  • voltmeterAn instrument that measures potential difference and is connected in parallel.
  • ammeterAn instrument that measures electric current and is connected in series.
  • resistanceThe property of a conductor that resists the flow of charges.
  • ohmThe SI unit of resistance.
  • resistivityA constant property of a material that helps decide its resistance.
  • series combinationA joining of resistors end to end so the same current flows through each.
  • parallel combinationA joining of resistors between the same two points so current divides.
  • heating effect of electric currentThe production of heat when current flows through a resistor.
  • fuseA safety wire that melts and breaks the circuit when current is too high.
