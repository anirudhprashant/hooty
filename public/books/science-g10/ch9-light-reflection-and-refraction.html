Words this chapter explains

  • ReflectionLight bouncing back from a surface such as a mirror.
  • RefractionChange in direction of light when it travels obliquely from one medium to another.
  • Ray of lightA straight-line path along which light travels.
  • Opaque objectAn object that casts a sharp shadow when light falls on it.
  • Virtual imageAn image that cannot be obtained on a screen.
  • Erect imageAn image that is upright, not upside down.
  • Laterally invertedAn image in which left and right sides are swapped.
  • Spherical mirrorA mirror whose reflecting surface is part of a sphere.
  • Concave mirrorA spherical mirror whose reflecting surface curves inwards.
  • Convex mirrorA spherical mirror whose reflecting surface curves outwards.
  • Centre of curvatureThe centre of the sphere of which a spherical mirror forms a part.
  • Principal focusThe point on the principal axis where parallel rays meet or appear to come from after reflection.
  • Focal lengthThe distance between the pole and the principal focus of a spherical mirror.
  • Refractive indexThe ratio of the speed of light in one medium to its speed in another, or the constant in Snell's law.
  • Power of a lensThe reciprocal of the focal length of a lens.
