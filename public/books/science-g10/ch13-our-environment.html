Words this chapter explains

  • ecosystemAll living organisms in an area together with non-living things, forming a balanced system.
  • biotic componentsThe living parts of an ecosystem, such as plants and animals.
  • abiotic componentsThe non-living physical parts of an ecosystem, like temperature and soil.
  • producersGreen plants and certain bacteria that make food by photosynthesis.
  • consumersOrganisms that eat food made by producers, directly or indirectly.
  • decomposersMicroorganisms like bacteria and fungi that break down dead remains and waste.
  • food chainA series of organisms in which each one feeds on the one before it.
  • trophic levelEach step or level in a food chain where organisms obtain energy.
  • biological magnificationThe build-up of harmful non-degradable chemicals at higher trophic levels.
  • biodegradableSubstances that can be broken down by biological processes.
  • non-biodegradableSubstances that are not broken down by biological processes.
  • ozoneA molecule of three oxygen atoms that shields Earth from UV radiation.
  • chlorofluorocarbonsSynthetic chemicals used in refrigerants and fire extinguishers that deplete ozone.
  • ultraviolet radiationHigh-energy radiation from the Sun that can damage organisms.
