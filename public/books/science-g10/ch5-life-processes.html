Words this chapter explains

  • life processesMaintenance processes that together keep a living organism alive.
  • nutritionProcess of taking a source of energy from outside into the body.
  • autotrophsOrganisms that use simple food material from inorganic sources.
  • heterotrophsOrganisms that use complex substances made by other organisms.
  • enzymesBio-catalysts that help break down complex substances.
  • photosynthesisProcess by which autotrophs convert carbon dioxide and water into carbohydrates using sunlight and chlorophyll.
  • chlorophyllGreen pigment that absorbs light energy for photosynthesis.
  • stomataTiny pores on leaf surface for gas exchange.
  • salivaFluid secreted by salivary glands that wets food.
  • villiFinger-like projections in small intestine that increase surface area for absorption.
  • respirationProcess of taking in oxygen and using it to break down food for cells.
  • aerobic respirationBreakdown of pyruvate using oxygen in mitochondria.
  • anaerobic respirationBreakdown of pyruvate without oxygen, as in yeast fermentation.
  • alveoliBalloon-like structures in lungs where gas exchange takes place.
  • haemoglobinRespiratory pigment in red blood corpuscles that carries oxygen.
  • excretionRemoval of harmful metabolic wastes from the body.
