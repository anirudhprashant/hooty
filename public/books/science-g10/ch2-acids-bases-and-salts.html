Words this chapter explains

  • acidA substance that tastes sour and turns blue litmus red.
  • baseA substance that tastes bitter and turns red litmus blue.
  • indicatorA dye or mixture that shows if a substance is acidic or basic by colour change.
  • litmusA natural purple dye from lichen used as an indicator.
  • olfactory indicatorsSubstances whose odour changes in acidic or basic media.
  • neutralisation reactionReaction between an acid and a base to give salt and water.
  • saltCompound formed when an acid reacts with a metal, base or carbonate.
  • pH scaleScale for measuring hydrogen ion concentration in a solution.
  • acid rainRain water with pH less than 5.6.
  • antacidA base medicine used to neutralise excess stomach acid.
  • dilutionMixing acid or base with water to decrease ion concentration.
  • water of crystallisationFixed number of water molecules in one formula unit of a salt.
  • bleaching powderA powder made by chlorine acting on dry slaked lime.
  • baking sodaCommon kitchen chemical, NaHCO3, used in cooking and antacids.
  • washing sodaBasic salt Na2CO3.10H2O used in industries and cleaning.
