Words this chapter explains

  • metallic lustreThe shining surface of a pure metal.
  • malleabilityThe property of a metal to be beaten into thin sheets.
  • ductilityThe ability of a metal to be drawn into thin wires.
  • sonorousMetals that make a sound when struck on a hard surface.
  • allotropeDifferent forms in which a non-metal like carbon can exist.
  • amphoteric oxidesMetal oxides that react with both acids and bases to form salts and water.
  • reactivity seriesA list of metals arranged from most reactive to least reactive.
  • ionic compoundsCompounds formed by transfer of electrons from a metal to a non-metal.
  • mineralA natural element or compound found in the earth's crust.
  • oreA mineral from which a metal can be extracted profitably.
  • gangueImpurities like soil and sand present in an ore.
  • roastingHeating a sulphide ore strongly in excess air to turn it into an oxide.
  • calcinationHeating a carbonate ore strongly in limited air to turn it into an oxide.
  • alloyA homogeneous mixture of two or more metals, or a metal and a non-metal.
