Words this chapter explains

  • chemical reactionA process where a chemical change takes place and new substances form.
  • reactantA starting substance that undergoes chemical change in a reaction.
  • productA new substance formed during a chemical reaction.
  • balanced chemical equationAn equation with equal numbers of atoms of each element on both sides.
  • combination reactionA reaction where two or more reactants form one product.
  • exothermic reactionA reaction that releases heat along with products.
  • decomposition reactionA reaction where one reactant breaks down into simpler products.
  • endothermic reactionA reaction that absorbs energy.
  • displacement reactionA reaction where an element displaces another element from its compound.
  • double displacement reactionA reaction where ions are exchanged between reactants.
  • precipitateAn insoluble solid formed in a solution during a reaction.
  • oxidationGain of oxygen or loss of hydrogen by a substance.
  • reductionLoss of oxygen or gain of hydrogen by a substance.
  • redox reactionA reaction where one substance is oxidised and another is reduced.
  • corrosionAttack on a metal by substances around it, such as moisture or acids.
  • rancidityCondition when fats and oils are oxidised and smell or taste changes.
