Words this chapter explains

  • treeA tall woody plant with a trunk, branches and leaves.
  • trunkThe thick wooden main part of a tree.
  • branchesTree limbs that spread out and have leaves on them.
  • rootsParts of a tree that grow deep down into the soil.
  • shrubA medium-sized plant with several woody stems near the ground.
  • woodyHard like wood, as in the stems of a shrub.
  • herbA smaller plant with soft stems that do not become woody.
  • grassA type of herb with thin, flat leaves and hollow stems.
  • climberA plant that climbs on another plant for support.
  • creeperA plant that grows along the ground.
  • leavesPlant parts that can differ in colour, shape, size, texture and smell.
  • stemA plant part that carries branches and leaves.
  • flowersPlant parts that can appear on a plant.
  • fruitsParts of a plant that can appear and have different smells.
  • seedsParts of shrubs and large grasses that can be eaten as pulses or grains.
  • barkThe hard outer covering of a tree trunk.
