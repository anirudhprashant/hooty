  • wasteWaste is something that people throw away after using it.
  • garbage pilesGarbage piles are heaps of waste left in places.
  • manage wasteManaging waste means controlling waste properly.
  • wastewaterWastewater is water that has been used and carries waste.
  • reduceReduce means to make something smaller or less.
  • reuseReuse means to use something again instead of throwing it away.
  • resourcesResources are useful materials that we can use again.
  • localityA locality is a small area such as a village or neighbourhood.
  • zero waste villageA zero waste village manages its waste so well that it receives an award.
  • compostCompost is food made from waste that rots and mixes with soil.
  • recyclingRecycling means making new objects from discarded materials.
  • National Cleanliness DayNational Cleanliness Day is celebrated on 30 January.
  • Cleanliness DriveA Cleanliness Drive encourages people to keep places clean and share things.
