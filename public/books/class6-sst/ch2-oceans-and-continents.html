Words this chapter explains

  • OceanThe biggest bodies of salty water on Earth.
  • LandmassA large body of land.
  • ContinentA very large, continuous stretch of land.
  • IslandA piece of land with water on all sides. It is smaller than a continent.
  • FreshwaterWater that is not salty. It is found in glaciers, rivers, lakes and under the ground.
  • GroundwaterFresh water that lies under the ground.
  • MarineFound in, or to do with, the oceans and seas.
  • FloraThe plant life of a place.
  • FaunaThe animal life of a place.
  • TsunamiA huge, powerful wave in the ocean, usually caused by an earthquake or a volcano under the sea.
  • CycloneA violent storm from the ocean with very strong winds and heavy rain.
  • EurasiaEurope and Asia seen together as one continent.
  • Disaster managementThe work of handling events in which people lose their lives or their property.
  • Water cycleThe way water moves from the oceans to rain on the land and back again.
  • OverfishingCatching far too many fish.
