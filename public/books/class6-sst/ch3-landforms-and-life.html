Words this chapter explains

  • landformA physical feature on Earth’s surface, such as a mountain, plateau or plain.
  • altitudeThe height of an object above sea level.
  • precipitationWater that reaches the ground from the atmosphere in forms such as rain, snow or hail.
  • mountain rangeA group of mountains, such as the Himalayas or the Andes.
  • summitThe highest and narrow top of a mountain.
  • erosionThe wearing away of mountains, making older mountains more rounded.
  • upliftmentThe raising of mountains, which may continue at the same time as erosion.
  • montane forestA forest that grows in mountainous regions.
  • tributaryA river that flows into another river.
  • valleyA low area between hills or mountains.
  • plateauRaised land with a surface that is more or less flat.
  • lava plateauA plateau formed when volcanic activity creates it.
  • sedimentParticles of rock, sand and silt carried and deposited by rivers.
  • irrigationSupplying water to fields through canals or from groundwater.
