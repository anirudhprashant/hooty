Words this chapter explains

  • SubcontinentThe region of the world that contains India and nearby lands.
  • inhabitantsPeople who live in a particular place.
  • Ṛig VedaIndia's oldest text, which is several thousand years old.
  • Sapta SindhavaThe name given to the north-western region in the Ṛig Veda.
  • SindhuAn old word for the Indus River or for a river in general.
  • MahābhārataOne of India's most famous ancient texts.
  • BhāratavarṣhaA name for the whole Indian Subcontinent used in the Mahābhārata.
  • JambudvīpaAn old name that came to mean the Indian Subcontinent.
  • BharataA name first used for a Vedic group and later for the Indian Subcontinent.
  • jamun treeA common tree native to India whose fruit gives Jambudvīpa its meaning.
  • ConstitutionA document that states a nation's basic principles and laws.
  • PersiansAncient people from Iran who were the first foreigners to mention India.
  • HinduAn ancient Persian geographical name for the land of the Indus.
  • IndoiA name used by the ancient Greeks for the region.
  • XuanzangA Chinese traveller who visited India and collected Buddhist texts.
  • HindustānA name first recorded in a Persian inscription about 1,800 years ago.
