Words this chapter explains

  • Economic activitiesActivities that involve money or payment in money’s worth.
  • MarketA place where people exchange goods and services.
  • FeeA payment made for professional advice or services.
  • Money’s worthThe monetary value that a person gives to an object because of its benefit.
  • Non-economic activitiesActivities that do not generate income or wealth but are done out of feelings such as love and care.
  • SalaryA fixed payment that an employer usually gives an employee every month.
  • WageA payment made by an employer to a worker for a fixed period.
  • Payment in kindA non-cash payment, such as mangoes, received for work.
  • Value additionAdding value while changing something into another form.
  • PrasādA sacred offering distributed to devotees at a temple.
  • LangarA place where free food is served to every visitor.
  • Swachh Bharat AbhiyanA programme based on citizens working together to keep India clean.
