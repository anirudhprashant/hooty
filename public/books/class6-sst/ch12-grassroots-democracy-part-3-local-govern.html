Words this chapter explains

  • participatory democracyA system in which citizens take an active part in decisions and governance.
  • decentralisedIt means giving local communities a direct say instead of leaving all decisions to a central authority.
  • urban local bodiesThese are the local government structures that manage urban areas.
  • wardA ward is a smaller area into which a city or town is divided.
  • ward committeeA ward committee organises local activities and reports local problems to the authorities.
  • infrastructureInfrastructure includes basic services and facilities such as roads, drains and water pipes.
  • waste segregationWaste segregation means keeping different kinds of rubbish separate.
  • burial groundA burial ground is a place where dead bodies are buried.
  • municipal institutionA municipal institution is an organisation that manages local city affairs.
  • charterA charter here is an official document that grants the right to form a corporation.
  • municipal taxA municipal tax is a local tax used by a city’s municipal body.
  • Municipal CorporationIt is the highest urban local body in a city with a population above 10 lakhs.
  • Municipal CouncilIt is the highest urban local body in a city with a population between 1 and 10 lakhs.
  • Nagar PanchayatIt is the urban local body used in cities and towns with smaller populations.
  • Citizen Relationship ManagementCRM is the name used for citizen-focused municipal services in the chapter.
  • local governmentLocal government manages the needs and affairs of a particular area such as a city.
