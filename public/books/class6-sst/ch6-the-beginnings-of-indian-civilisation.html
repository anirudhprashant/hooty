Words this chapter explains

  • Indus-Sarasvatī CivilisationThe early Indian civilisation whose people were called Harappans.
  • civilisationAn advanced stage of human society with organised work, towns, trade and government.
  • urbanismThe planning, growth and management of cities.
  • metallurgyThe work of extracting, purifying, combining and studying metals.
  • fortificationA strong wall built around a settlement or city for protection.
  • eliteThe higher social group, including rulers, officials, administrators and often priests.
  • reservoirA natural or made place where a large amount of water is stored.
  • pulsesCrops such as beans, peas and lentils.
  • ploughA farming tool that Harappans used for working soil.
  • steatiteA soft stone that can be hardened by heating.
  • writing systemA set of signs used for writing, although the Harappan signs are not fully understood.
  • upper town and lower townTwo parts of a Harappan city, with the elite above and common people below.
