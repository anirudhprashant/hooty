Words this chapter explains

  • SocietyThe family is called the fundamental and most ancient unit of society.
  • Joint familyA family in which several generations live together.
  • Nuclear familyA family made up mainly of a couple and their children.
  • Family relationshipsThe connections between relatives, for which languages use different terms.
  • CooperationWorking together with other people.
  • InterdependenceDepending on one another for support, work and shared duties.
  • DānaA family value that means giving.
  • SevāA family value that means service.
  • TyāgaA family value that means sacrifice.
  • HouseholdThe shared home life in which children take part.
  • CommunityA group of connected people who come together for shared reasons and duties.
  • HalmaA Bhil tradition of supporting anyone in a crisis and serving Mother Earth.
  • JātiA group or subdivision that can also be treated as a community.
  • Residents’ Welfare AssociationsUrban communities that make their own rules and regulations.
