Words this chapter explains

  • unicellularAn organism made from only one cell, such as a bacterium.
  • multicellularAn organism made from many cells that work together.
  • resolutionA measure of how clear and distinct an image appears.
  • cell membraneA thin boundary that surrounds and protects a cell.
  • osmosisThe movement of water across a selectively permeable membrane.
  • diffusionThe net movement of particles from higher to lower concentration.
  • cell wallA rigid, permeable extra layer outside the membrane of some cells.
  • cytoplasmA semi-fluid, jelly-like substance that fills a cell.
  • organelleA specialised structure inside a cell that performs a particular job.
  • prokaryotic cellA cell without a well-defined nucleus or membrane-bound organelles.
  • eukaryotic cellA cell with a well-defined nucleus and membrane-bound organelles.
  • mitochondrionAn organelle that supplies energy for most cellular activities.
  • vacuoleA membrane-bound space that stores water, minerals, sugars and waste.
  • mitosisCell division that produces two genetically identical daughter cells.
