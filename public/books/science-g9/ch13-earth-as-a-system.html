Words this chapter explains

  • Earth systemThe whole Earth viewed as connected parts that interact with one another.
  • GeosphereThe solid part of Earth, including rocks, soil, landforms and the interior.
  • HydrosphereAll of Earth’s liquid water, including oceans, rivers, lakes and groundwater.
  • CryosphereThe frozen part of Earth, including ice, snow and glaciers.
  • AtmosphereThe layer of gases surrounding Earth.
  • BiosphereAll living organisms and the places where they live.
  • Electromagnetic radiationEnergy waves that travel through space as electric and magnetic waves.
  • InsolationThe amount of solar radiation that reaches Earth’s surface.
  • TroposphereThe lowest atmospheric layer, where most weather occurs.
  • Ozone layerThe atmospheric layer that absorbs harmful ultraviolet radiation.
  • Greenhouse effectThe warming caused when greenhouse gases trap outgoing heat.
  • Planetary windsLarge-scale winds that move over long distances between pressure belts.
  • Ocean currentsContinuous movements of large masses of ocean water.
  • Biogeochemical cycleThe cyclic movement of matter and energy between living and non-living parts of Earth.
  • EutrophicationExcessive algal growth that uses up oxygen in water and can kill fish.
