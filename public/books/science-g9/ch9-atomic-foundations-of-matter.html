Words this chapter explains

  • physical changeA change in which no new substance is formed, such as salt dissolving in water.
  • chemical changeA change in which new substances are formed during a reaction.
  • solutionA mixture in which salt spreads evenly through water.
  • Law of Conservation of MassThe rule that matter is neither created nor destroyed during a chemical reaction.
  • Law of Constant ProportionsThe rule that elements combine in a fixed ratio by mass in a compound.
  • postulateA fundamental idea accepted as truth from which further ideas are developed.
  • moleculeAn electrically neutral particle made from more than one bonded atom.
  • chemical bondThe force that holds atoms together in a substance.
  • covalent bondA bond formed when atoms share one or more pairs of electrons.
  • ionic bondThe attraction between oppositely charged ions in an ionic compound.
  • ionAn atom or group of atoms that has lost or gained electrons.
  • cationA positively charged particle formed when an atom loses electrons.
  • anionA negatively charged particle formed when an atom gains electrons.
  • formula unitThe simplest whole-number ratio of ions in an ionic compound.
  • molecular massThe total mass of all the atoms present in one molecule.
  • formula unit massThe mass of the simplest whole-number ratio of ions in an ionic compound.
