Words this chapter explains

  • matterThe substance that makes up the things we can see, observe or feel.
  • atomTiny particles that make up matter.
  • dravyaThe Sanskrit word used by Acharya Kanada for matter.
  • parmanuThe smallest particle in Kanada’s theory that could not be divided.
  • dyad and triadGroups of two and three parmanus that combine to form larger things.
  • atomosThe Greek word meaning indivisible.
  • radioactivityA phenomenon in which some elements emit invisible energy and particles.
  • cathode rayRays travelling from the negative cathode towards the positive anode in Thomson’s tube.
  • electronA tiny negatively charged particle emitted from atoms.
  • nucleusA tiny central region containing positive charge and most of an atom’s mass.
  • alpha particleA tiny positively charged particle emitted by some radioactive elements.
  • stationary stateA fixed electron path with definite energy in Bohr’s model.
  • neutronA neutral subatomic particle found in all nuclei except hydrogen.
  • atomic numberThe number of protons in the nucleus of an atom of an element.
  • mass numberThe total number of protons and neutrons in an atom’s nucleus.
  • valencyThe number of electrons gained, lost or shared to complete an octet.
