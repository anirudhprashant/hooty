Words this chapter explains

  • forceA push or pull that can change an object's motion or shape.
  • newtonThe SI unit used for measuring force.
  • spring balanceAn instrument that can be used to measure the magnitude of a force.
  • frictionA force that acts opposite to motion or attempted motion.
  • balanced forcesTwo forces that are equal in magnitude and opposite in direction.
  • net forceThe combined effect of all the forces acting on an object.
  • normal forceThe upward force exerted by a surface on an object placed on it.
  • inertiaThe tendency of an object to resist changes in its rest or uniform motion.
  • momentumThe product of an object's mass and velocity.
  • accelerationThe change in an object's velocity.
  • massMass affects acceleration when the same force acts on an object.
  • tensionThe pulling force transmitted through a connecting string.
  • systemConnected objects that can be studied as one whole.
  • external forceA force acting on a system from outside that system.
