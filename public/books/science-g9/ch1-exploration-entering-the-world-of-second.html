Words this chapter explains

  • modelA simplified way to study a real system by keeping only important details.
  • assumptionAn idea accepted while building a model, even when some details are ignored.
  • air resistanceAn effect of air on a moving object that a simple model may leave out.
  • unitA defined standard used to express the value of a quantity.
  • SI unitA standard measurement unit that helps scientists avoid conversion errors.
  • equationA compact statement showing how certain quantities or things are related.
  • lawA description of a regular pattern that is observed in nature.
  • theoryAn evidence-based explanation of why a scientific pattern occurs.
  • predictionA reasoned expectation about what may happen, based on evidence.
  • estimateA rough answer used to check whether a result is reasonable.
  • disciplineAn area of science, such as physics, chemistry, biology, or earth science.
  • scientific languageA shared set of precise scientific terms, symbols, and units.
