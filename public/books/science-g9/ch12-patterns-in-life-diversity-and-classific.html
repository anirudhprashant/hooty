Words this chapter explains

  • biodiversityThe immense variety of living organisms in different forms and habitats.
  • endemic speciesA species found naturally only in a particular region of the world.
  • biodiversity hotspotA region with many endemic species that has suffered significant habitat loss.
  • classificationA systematic way of organising living diversity by shared characteristics.
  • biological classificationThe scientific system used to group living organisms.
  • eukaryoteAn organism whose cell has a membrane-bound true nucleus.
  • autotrophicA mode of nutrition in which an organism makes its own food.
  • heterotrophicA mode of nutrition in which an organism depends on other organisms for food.
  • saprophyteAn organism that feeds on dead organic matter.
  • notochordA flexible rod-shaped structure found in some chordates.
  • invertebrateAn animal without a notochord.
  • vertebrateAn animal with a vertebral column or backbone.
  • binomial nomenclatureThe system of giving each organism a two-part scientific name.
  • domainOne of the three large groups in the three-domain system: Bacteria, Archaea or Eukarya.
  • fossilA preserved remain of a plant or animal that records life from earlier times.
