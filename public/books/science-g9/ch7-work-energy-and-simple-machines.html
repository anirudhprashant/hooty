Words this chapter explains

  • workWork done when a force causes displacement in its direction.
  • jouleThe SI unit of work done and energy.
  • energyThe capacity an object has to do work.
  • kinetic energyThe energy possessed by an object because of its motion.
  • potential energyEnergy stored because of deformation or the positions of objects.
  • mechanical energyThe sum of an object’s kinetic energy and potential energy.
  • conservation of mechanical energyMechanical energy remains the same when no other external force acts.
  • powerThe rate at which work is done.
  • simple machineA device that makes work easier by changing force magnitude or direction.
  • mechanical advantageThe ratio of the load to the effort applied.
  • effortThe force that a person applies to a machine.
  • loadThe force that must be overcome by a machine.
  • pulleyA grooved wheel that guides a rope.
  • inclined planeA sloping surface used to raise or lower a load.
  • leverA rigid bar that turns about a fixed point.
