Words this chapter explains

  • homogeneous mixtureA mixture with a uniform composition throughout.
  • heterogeneous mixtureA mixture whose components are not uniformly distributed.
  • soluteThe substance that gets dissolved in a solvent.
  • solventThe substance that dissolves a solute.
  • solubilityThe maximum amount of solute that dissolves in a fixed quantity of solvent.
  • saturated solutionA solution that cannot dissolve any more solute at a given temperature.
  • crystallizationThe process of forming crystals from a saturated solution.
  • distillationA separation method in which a liquid vaporises and is then cooled into a liquid.
  • paper chromatographyA method that separates mixture components by their movement on paper.
  • immiscible liquidsLiquids that do not mix and form separate layers.
  • sublimationThe direct change of a solid into vapour without passing through a liquid.
  • suspensionA mixture in which undissolved solid particles remain suspended in a medium.
  • centrifugationA separation method that spins a mixture so heavier particles move outwards.
  • coagulationA process in which small particles clump together and settle.
  • colloidA mixture whose particles are intermediate in size and remain dispersed.
  • Tyndall effectThe scattering of light by particles in a colloid or suspension.
