Words this chapter explains

  • reproductionThis is the biological process by which living beings produce new individuals of their own kind.
  • asexual reproductionThis is reproduction involving one parent and producing nearly identical offspring.
  • sexual reproductionThis is reproduction in which two parents contribute genetic material to the offspring.
  • vegetative propagationNew plants grow from the vegetative or growing parts of a parent plant.
  • buddingA bud forms on the parent body, grows and then separates to live independently.
  • sporeA tiny reproductive structure that can grow into a new organism under suitable conditions.
  • cloneAn offspring that is genetically identical to its parent.
  • meiosisThis special cell division forms gametes with half the parent cell’s chromosome number.
  • chromosomeA thread-like structure in a cell nucleus that carries genetic information.
  • gameteA reproductive cell that joins with another gamete during fertilisation.
  • fertilisationThis is the joining of male and female gametes to begin new life.
  • zygoteThis is the first cell formed when the male and female gametes fuse.
  • external fertilisationThis is fertilisation that takes place outside the female’s body.
  • menstruationThis is the monthly shedding of the thick uterine lining when fertilisation does not occur.
