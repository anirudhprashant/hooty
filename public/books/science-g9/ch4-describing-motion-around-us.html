Words this chapter explains

  • linear motionMotion in which an object moves along a straight line.
  • reference pointA fixed point used to describe an object's position.
  • originThe marked reference point from which positions are measured.
  • instant of timeA single reading of the clock at a particular point of time.
  • time intervalThe time between two instants of time.
  • distance travelledThe total length of the path covered by an object.
  • displacementThe net change in an object's position between two instants.
  • magnitudeThe numerical value, with units, of a physical quantity.
  • scalarA physical quantity described using only its numerical value.
  • vectorA physical quantity that needs both magnitude and direction.
  • average speedTotal distance travelled divided by the time interval.
  • average velocityChange in position divided by the time interval, with direction.
  • rate of changeThe change in one quantity compared with the corresponding change in time.
  • instantaneous velocityThe velocity of an object at a particular instant.
  • average accelerationChange in velocity divided by the time interval.
  • uniform circular motionCircular motion in which the speed stays constant.
