Words this chapter explains

  • sourceThe object that produces a sound.
  • tuning forkA U-shaped metal instrument whose prongs vibrate and produce sound.
  • mediumA material through which sound can travel.
  • vacuumA space containing no matter, in which sound cannot travel.
  • compressionA region of a medium where the particles are closer together.
  • rarefactionA region of a medium where the particles are more spread out.
  • longitudinal waveA wave in which particles vibrate parallel to the wave’s direction.
  • mechanical waveA wave that needs a material medium in which to travel.
  • crestThe highest point of a sound-wave graph.
  • troughThe lowest point of a sound-wave graph.
  • wavelengthThe distance between two consecutive crests or two consecutive troughs.
  • frequencyThe number of complete density oscillations at a fixed point per unit time.
  • time periodThe time taken to complete one density oscillation at a fixed point.
  • echolocationThe use of reflected sound waves to find the position of objects.
