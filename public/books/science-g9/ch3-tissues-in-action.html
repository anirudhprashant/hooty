Words this chapter explains

  • tissueA group of similar cells that works together for a specific function.
  • multicellular organismAn organism made from many cells with different jobs.
  • unicellular organismAn organism in which one cell performs all the functions of life.
  • division of labourThe sharing of different functions among different groups of cells.
  • meristematic tissueA tissue whose cells divide actively and add new cells to the plant.
  • apical meristemMeristem at root and shoot tips that increases a plant's length.
  • lateral meristemMeristem arranged around a stem that increases its girth.
  • intercalary meristemMeristem that helps plants regrow after cutting or grazing.
  • differentiationThe change by which meristematic cells become specialised permanent cells.
  • permanent tissueA tissue of specialised cells that no longer keeps dividing.
  • xylemA complex plant tissue that carries water and minerals and gives strength.
  • phloemA complex plant tissue that mainly carries food made in leaves.
  • epidermisThe tightly packed outer layer that protects the plant.
  • sclerenchymaA simple permanent tissue with thick lignin-containing walls.
  • neuronA nerve cell that receives, processes and transmits messages.
  • jointA junction between bones that allows movement.
