Words this chapter explains

  • perimeterThe total distance covered around the outside boundary of a closed shape.
  • closed plane figureA shape that encloses a region and has a complete boundary.
  • polygonA closed plane figure made from straight line segments.
  • boundaryThe outer edge along which a closed figure is measured.
  • squareA closed figure with all four sides equal in length.
  • triangleA closed figure made of three sides.
  • regular polygonA closed figure in which all sides and all angles are equal.
  • equilateral triangleA triangle in which all three sides and all three angles are equal.
  • graph paperPaper marked with squares for estimating area.
  • breadthThe shorter measurement of a rectangle in the examples.
