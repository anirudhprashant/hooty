Words this chapter explains

  • geometryIt includes ideas involving points, lines, rays, line segments and angles.
  • pointAn exact place with no length, breadth or height.
  • line segmentThe shortest path joining two points, including both ends.
  • lineA line segment extended without an end in both directions.
  • starting pointThe point from which a ray begins.
  • angleTwo rays with a common starting point.
  • rotationA turn of one ray about the vertex towards the other ray.
  • superimpositionPlacing one angle over another so their parts overlap.
  • protractorA tool used to measure angles in degrees.
  • straight angleAn angle made by a half turn, measuring 180°.
  • right angleAn angle that is half a straight angle, measuring 90°.
  • acute angleAn angle greater than 0° but less than 90°.
  • obtuse angleAn angle greater than 90° but less than 180°.
