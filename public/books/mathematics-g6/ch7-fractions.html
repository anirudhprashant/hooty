Words this chapter explains

  • fractionA fraction tells how much of a whole is in an equal share.
  • equal shareAn equal share is one of the equal parts made when a whole is shared.
  • fractional unitA fractional unit is one part when one whole unit is divided equally.
  • numeratorThe numerator is the top number of a fraction.
  • denominatorThe denominator is the bottom number of a fraction.
  • mixed numberA mixed number contains a whole number and a fraction smaller than one.
  • whole partThe whole part is the whole number in a mixed number.
  • fractional partThe fractional part is the fraction smaller than one in a mixed number.
  • number lineA number line has a point associated with every fraction.
  • equivalent fractionsEquivalent fractions represent the same share or number.
  • lowest termsIn lowest terms, the numerator and denominator have no common factor except 1.
  • common denominatorA common denominator is the same denominator used for equivalent fractions.
  • Egyptian fractionsEgyptian fractions express a number as a sum of fractional units.
  • bhinnaBhinna is the Sanskrit word meaning broken.
  • bhagaBhaga and an sha mean part or piece.
