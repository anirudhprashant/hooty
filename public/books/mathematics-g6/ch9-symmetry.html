Words this chapter explains

  • symmetryA pattern in which parts of a figure repeat in a definite way.
  • symmetricalA figure that has symmetry.
  • asymmetricalA figure that is not symmetrical.
  • repeating patternParts that come back again in the same planned order.
  • line of symmetryA line that divides a figure into two exactly overlapping parts.
  • axis of symmetryAnother name for a line of symmetry.
  • mirror halvesTwo parts that match exactly when one is folded over the other.
  • reflectionSending one side of a figure to the other side across a line.
  • reflection symmetrySymmetry shown by folding a figure along a line.
  • centre of rotationThe fixed point about which a figure is rotated.
  • angle of symmetryAn angle through which a figure can turn and look exactly the same.
  • rotational symmetrySymmetry shown when a figure looks the same after turning about a fixed point.
  • radial armsArms that spread out from the centre of a figure.
  • diameterA line through a circle's centre that reaches the rim on both sides.
