Words this chapter explains

  • money givenThis is the money Rupal’s mother gave her for the fair.
  • money spentThis is the money used on rides, snacks or other things.
  • money leftThis is the money still kept after some money has been spent.
  • currencyThese are the notes and coins used in India.
  • noteThis is a piece of paper money that shows its value.
  • coinThis is a small round piece of money.
  • paiseThis is a small unit of money, and 100 paise make one rupee.
  • specially abled peopleThese are the people for whom a special number is printed on notes.
  • targetThis is the balloon that Matru aims to pop to win money.
  • combinationThis is a group of notes and coins that adds to a wanted amount.
  • amountThis is a stated quantity of money to pay or win.
  • total amount wonThis is the full amount of money that Matru wins.
  • itemsThese are the things that can be bought at the fair.
  • mobile phonesThis is a device that can sometimes be used to pay.
  • saving moneyThis means keeping some money instead of spending all of it.
