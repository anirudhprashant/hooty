Words this chapter explains

  • asideOut of the way, as the friends move.
  • sometimesAt one time and not at another time.
  • trackThe way or path the bicycle stays on.
  • helmetThe little safety item the child puts on before riding.
  • everywhereTo every place the child goes.
  • bellThe bicycle part that makes the ringing sound.
  • airThe thing the child says is needed for the bicycle to keep moving.
  • tyresThe black bicycle parts mentioned beside the pedals.
  • wheelchairThe chair that some people need to use.
