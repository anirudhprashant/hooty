Words this chapter explains

  • crowThe crow is the black bird who tries to look beautiful.
  • beautifulBeautiful means lovely to see or having good qualities.
  • colourfulColourful means having many bright colours.
  • featherA feather is the colourful find that the crow puts on his body.
  • peacockA peacock is the bird whose feather the crow finds.
  • foolThe other crows call the dressed-up crow a fool.
  • loveLove means liking the crow for what he is.
  • skin toneA skin tone is the colour of a person's skin.
  • complexionA complexion is another word for skin tone.
  • qualityA quality is a good feature of a person.
  • uniquenessUniqueness means being different from other animals or birds.
  • syllableA syllable is a small part of a word that children can clap for.
  • rhymeA rhyme is a matching word sound, such as grow and crow.
