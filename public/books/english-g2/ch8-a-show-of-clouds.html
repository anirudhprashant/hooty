Words this chapter explains

  • cloudsClouds are shapes in the sky that the child watches and imagines.
  • skyThe sky is the place above the child where clouds are seen.
  • lieTo lie means to rest with the body down.
  • backThe back is the body side on which the child is resting.
  • bearA bear is an animal that the child imagines in the clouds.
  • shipA ship is a boat that the child imagines in the clouds.
  • seaThe sea is the white area below the ship in the child’s cloud picture.
  • sailingSailing means moving over the sea, as the ship does in the poem.
  • elephantAn elephant is an animal that the child imagines in the clouds.
  • trunkThe trunk is the part of the elephant that waves at the child.
  • wavingWaving means moving the trunk, as the elephant does in the poem.
  • coloursColours are the names of the cloud colours to choose from.
  • alphabetical orderAlphabetical order is the order in which the letters of the alphabet come.
  • common soundA common sound is the same sound heard in many of the listed words.
