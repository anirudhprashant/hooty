Words this chapter explains

  • alimentary canalA long tube through which food passes as it is digested.
  • mechanical digestionThe first physical breakdown of food into smaller and finer pieces.
  • salivaA liquid in the mouth that moistens food and begins breaking down starch.
  • digestionThe breaking down of complex food components into simpler forms in the body.
  • oesophagusThe tube that carries swallowed food from the mouth to the stomach.
  • bileA mildly basic secretion made by the liver that helps digest fats.
  • pancreasAn organ that secretes pancreatic juice into the small intestine.
  • absorptionThe movement of digested nutrients into the blood through the small intestine.
  • egestionThe removal of undigested solid waste from the body through the anus.
  • ruminationBringing partly digested food back to the mouth for gradual chewing again.
  • breathingThe movement of air into and out of the lungs.
  • respiratory systemThe body system used for breathing and respiration.
  • alveoliTiny sacs in the lungs where gases pass between air and blood.
  • respirationThe chemical process in which nutrients are changed into usable energy.
  • circulatory systemThe system containing the heart, blood and blood vessels that transports substances around the body.
