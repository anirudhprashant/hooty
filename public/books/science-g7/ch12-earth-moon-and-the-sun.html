Words this chapter explains

  • axis of rotationAn imaginary line around which an object rotates.
  • revolutionThe movement of one object around another object.
  • orbitThe path followed by an object as it revolves around another object.
  • day-night cycleThe repeating change between daylight and darkness caused by rotation.
  • star trailsCurved arcs that record the apparent movement of stars in a long-exposure photograph.
  • apparent sizeHow large an object looks to the eye.
  • solar eclipseAn event in which the Moon blocks sunlight from reaching Earth.
  • total solar eclipseAn eclipse in which observers in the Moon’s shadow see no part of the Sun.
  • partial solar eclipseAn eclipse in which the Moon covers only some parts of the Sun.
  • lunar eclipseAn event in which the Earth blocks sunlight from reaching the Moon.
  • total lunar eclipseAn eclipse in which the whole Moon lies in the Earth’s shadow.
  • partial lunar eclipseAn eclipse in which only part of the Moon lies in the Earth’s shadow.
  • equinoxA day around which daytime lasts about 12 hours.
  • summer solsticeThe day around 21 June with the longest daylight in the Northern Hemisphere.
  • winter solsticeThe day around 22 December with the shortest daylight in the Northern Hemisphere.
