Words this chapter explains

  • Electric cellA portable source of electrical energy with two terminals.
  • TerminalOne of the two metal ends of a cell or lamp.
  • BatteryA combination of cells that supplies electrical energy to a device.
  • FilamentThe thin wire inside an incandescent lamp that glows when current passes through it.
  • Incandescent lampA lamp that gives light when its filament becomes hot and glows.
  • LEDA lamp that gives out light when current passes through it in the correct direction.
  • Electric currentThe flow of electricity through a complete path.
  • Electrical circuitA complete path through which electric current can flow.
  • Circuit diagramA drawing of an electrical circuit made using symbols.
  • ConductorA material through which electric current can flow easily.
  • InsulatorA material through which electric current cannot pass.
  • FusedA lamp with a broken filament that stops current from flowing.
  • Direct CurrentThe type of electricity supplied by batteries.
  • Alternating CurrentThe type of electricity supplied to wall sockets by power plants.
