Words this chapter explains

  • acidA substance that often tastes sour and turns blue litmus paper red.
  • baseA substance that turns red litmus paper blue and is generally slippery.
  • neutralIt is neither acidic nor basic.
  • litmusA natural substance obtained from lichens and used for testing acidity.
  • litmus paperPaper strips of litmus used to test the nature of substances.
  • acid-base indicatorA substance that shows different colours in acidic and basic solutions.
  • synthetic indicatorAn indicator made in a laboratory.
  • lichenA natural organism formed by a fungus and an alga living together.
  • lime waterA solution made by mixing lime with water, leaving it and then filtering it.
  • filtrateThe liquid that remains after solid material has been removed.
  • turmeric paperYellow paper made from turmeric that can test for basic substances.
  • olfactory indicatorA substance whose odour changes in an acidic or basic medium.
  • neutralisationA reaction in which an acid and a base cancel each other’s effect.
  • formic acidThe acidic liquid injected into the skin by a red ant.
  • calcium hydroxideThe substance dissolved in water to make lime water.
  • mortar and pestleTools used together to crush the rose petals.
