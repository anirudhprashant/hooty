Words this chapter explains

  • adolescenceThe period of growth and change between childhood and adulthood.
  • maturityThe stage reached when the body is developed enough to reproduce.
  • pubertyThe stage when the body changes to become an adult capable of reproduction.
  • secondary sexual characteristicsNatural signs of approaching adulthood that are not directly involved in reproduction.
  • voice boxA structure in the throat that helps us speak.
  • acneA skin condition in which small reddish pimples appear.
  • menstrual cycleA recurring natural process that includes blood discharge during menstruation.
  • menstruationThe part of the menstrual cycle when blood leaves the body.
  • reproductive healthThe health connected with the body’s ability to reproduce.
  • mood swingsEmotions that shift between energetic and happy feelings and feeling low.
  • substance abuseThe regular use of harmful substances because of a strong urge to take them again.
  • cyberbullyingHarassment through digital devices, messages, rumours or sharing information without consent.
  • hormonesChemicals made by the body that help control growth, development, mood and behaviour.
  • nutritionGetting enough suitable food to support growth, development and health.
