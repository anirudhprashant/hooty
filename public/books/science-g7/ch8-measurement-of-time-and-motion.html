Words this chapter explains

  • sundialA device that uses the Sun’s shadow to tell the time.
  • water clockA device that measures time using the flow of water.
  • hourglassA device that measures time as sand flows between two bulbs.
  • solar timeThe local time shown by a sundial according to the Sun.
  • Ghatika-yantraA water clock whose floating bowl sank to mark time.
  • pendulumA hanging bob that swings to and fro from a fixed support.
  • oscillationA repeating back-and-forth movement of a pendulum.
  • time periodThe time taken by a pendulum to complete one oscillation.
  • quartz clockA clock that uses the rapid vibrations of a quartz crystal.
  • average speedTotal distance covered divided by the total time taken.
  • linear motionMovement of an object along a straight line.
  • uniform linear motionStraight-line motion in which the speed stays constant.
  • non-uniform linear motionStraight-line motion in which the speed keeps changing.
  • speedometerAn instrument that shows the speed of a vehicle.
  • odometerAn instrument that measures the distance travelled by a vehicle.
