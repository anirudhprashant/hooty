Words this chapter explains

  • physical changeA physical change alters properties such as shape or state without forming a new substance.
  • chemical changeA chemical change forms one or more new substances.
  • chemical reactionIt is the process through which new substances form.
  • chemical equationIt is a short way of representing a chemical reaction.
  • insolubleIt does not dissolve in water.
  • combustionIt is a reaction with oxygen that produces heat and/or light.
  • combustible substanceIt is a substance that can undergo combustion.
  • fuelIt is another name for a combustible substance.
  • ignition temperatureIt is the lowest temperature at which a substance can catch fire.
  • reversible changeIt is a change whose original form can be brought back.
  • desirable changeIt is a change that is useful in a particular situation.
  • sedimentIt is sand, soil or stone that settles at a place.
  • weatheringIt is the physical and chemical breakdown of rocks that helps form soil.
  • erosionIt is the breaking down and movement of rock and soil by natural forces.
  • bioluminescenceIt is the production of light without heat by a living organism.
  • calcium carbonateIt is the new white-coloured substance formed when carbon dioxide reacts with lime water.
