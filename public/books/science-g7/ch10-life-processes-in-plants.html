Words this chapter explains

  • nutrientsNutrients are parts of food that support growth and health.
  • saplingA sapling is a small young plant.
  • chlorophyllChlorophyll is the green pigment that helps leaves capture sunlight.
  • starchStarch is a carbohydrate in which plants store food.
  • carbohydrateA carbohydrate is one of the nutrients found in food.
  • destarchTo destarch a plant means to remove its stored starch.
  • decolourisationDecolourisation removes colour so that a colour change is easier to see.
  • photosynthesisPhotosynthesis is the process by which plants prepare food in sunlight using chlorophyll.
  • stomataStomata are tiny pores on leaves that help gases enter and leave.
  • xylemXylem is a tube-like structure that transports water and minerals.
  • phloemPhloem is a tube-like structure that transports food through a plant.
  • glucoseGlucose is a simple carbohydrate produced during photosynthesis.
  • caustic sodaCaustic soda is a strong chemical that absorbs carbon dioxide from air.
  • mineralsMinerals are nutrients found in soil that help plants grow.
  • destarchedA destarched plant has had its stored starch removed.
