Words this chapter explains

  • Good conductorA material that allows heat to pass through it easily.
  • ConvectionHeat moves from one place to another through the movement of particles.
  • MediumA substance whose particles help carry heat during conduction or convection.
  • ExpandsTakes up more space when heated.
  • CondensationWater vapour cools and changes into water to form clouds.
  • PrecipitationRain, snow or hail that comes from clouds.
  • InfiltrationSurface water seeps through soil and rocks.
  • GroundwaterWater stored below the surface in pores and rock openings.
  • AquiferUnderground layers of sediments and rocks that store water.
  • Land breezeCooler air moves from the land towards the sea at night.
