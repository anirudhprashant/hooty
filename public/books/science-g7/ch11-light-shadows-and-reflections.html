Words this chapter explains

  • light pollutionIt is one reason given for the decreasing number of fireflies.
  • emitIt means to give out one's own light.
  • luminousAn object that gives out its own light.
  • non-luminousAn object that does not give out its own light.
  • transparentIt is a material through which light passes almost completely.
  • translucentIt is a material through which light passes partly.
  • opaqueIt is a material through which light does not pass.
  • plane mirrorIt is a mirror with a flat surface rather than a curved one.
  • erectAn erect image stands upright.
  • lateral inversionIt is the left-right reversal seen in a plane mirror image.
  • pinhole cameraIt is a device that forms an image using light passing through a tiny hole.
  • periscopeIt is a device that uses two plane mirrors to see objects that are not directly visible.
  • kaleidoscopeIt is a tube with mirrors that shows changing patterns made by reflections.
