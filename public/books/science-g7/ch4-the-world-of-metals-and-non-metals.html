Words this chapter explains

  • lustrousHaving a shiny surface.
  • metallic lustreThe shine shown by a metal.
  • malleabilityThe ability of a material to be beaten into a thin sheet.
  • brittleLikely to break into pieces when hammered.
  • ductilityThe ability of a material to be drawn into wires.
  • sonorityThe ability to produce a ringing sound.
  • conductionThe transfer of heat from one point to another within a material.
  • rustThe brown deposit that forms on iron in moist air.
  • rustingThe process by which rust forms on iron.
  • corrosionThe gradual damage of a metal surface caused by air, water or other substances.
  • galvanisationThe putting of a protective zinc layer on iron.
  • oxideA substance formed when oxygen reacts with a metal or non-metal.
  • elementA substance that cannot be broken down into simpler substances.
  • alloyA mixture of two or more metals, or a metal and a non-metal.
