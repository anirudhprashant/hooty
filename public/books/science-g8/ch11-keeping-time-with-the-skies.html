Words this chapter explains

  • phases of the MoonThese are the changing shapes of the Moon’s bright part seen from Earth.
  • waxingWaxing means that the Moon’s bright part is increasing.
  • waningWaning means that the Moon’s bright part is decreasing.
  • gibbous phaseA gibbous Moon shows more than half of its bright part.
  • crescent phaseA crescent Moon shows less than half of its bright part.
  • mean solar dayThis is the average time from one highest position of the Sun to the next.
  • lunar calendarA lunar calendar counts time mainly using the Moon’s cycle.
  • solar calendarA solar calendar keeps its months and years matched with the seasons.
  • luni-solar calendarA luni-solar calendar uses Moon phases and adjustments to match the seasons.
  • intercalary monthAn intercalary month is an extra month added to keep lunar and solar cycles together.
  • Amant calendarAn Amant month begins after the new Moon and ends on the new Moon.
  • Purnimant calendarA Purnimant month begins after the full Moon and ends on the full Moon.
  • sidereal yearA sidereal year is the time for the same stars to rise again at sunset.
