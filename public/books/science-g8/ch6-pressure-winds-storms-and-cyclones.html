Words this chapter explains

  • pascalA pascal is the SI unit of pressure.
  • millibarA millibar is a practical unit of air pressure equal to 100 Pa.
  • hectopascalA hectopascal is a unit of air pressure equal to 100 Pa.
  • gravityGravity is the force that makes a carried bag feel heavy.
  • atmospheric pressureAtmospheric pressure is the pressure of the air around us.
  • windWind is moving air produced by differences in air pressure.
  • sea breezeA sea breeze blows from the sea towards land during the day.
  • land breezeA land breeze blows from land towards sea at night.
  • static electric chargeStatic electric charges develop when strong winds rub particles together.
  • thunderstormA thunderstorm is a storm accompanied by lightning and thunder.
  • lightning conductorA lightning conductor gives electric charges an easy path into the ground.
  • cycloneA cyclone is a large storm formed over warm ocean water.
  • eye of cycloneThe eye of a cyclone is its calm centre.
