Words this chapter explains

  • magnetic effectThis is the magnetic field produced when electric current flows through a conductor.
  • magnetic fieldThis is the region around a magnet or current-carrying wire where its magnetic effect can be felt.
  • conductorA conductor is a material through which electric current can flow.
  • electromagnetAn electromagnet is a current-carrying coil that behaves like a magnet.
  • iron coreAn iron core is placed inside a coil to make the electromagnet stronger.
  • lifting electromagnetA lifting electromagnet is a strong electromagnet used to lift metal objects.
  • resistanceResistance is the opposition that a conductor offers to the flow of current.
  • heating effectThe heating effect is the warming of a conductor when electric current passes through it.
  • nichrome wireNichrome wire is a wire that offers more resistance than copper wire of the same size and length.
  • Voltaic cellA Voltaic cell makes electricity through a chemical reaction between two different metals and an electrolyte.
  • electrodeElectrodes are the partly dipped metal rods inside a cell.
  • dry cellA dry cell contains a thick moist paste instead of a liquid electrolyte.
  • rechargeable batteryA rechargeable battery can be charged again and used several times.
