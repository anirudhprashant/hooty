Words this chapter explains

  • lensA curved piece of glass that makes small objects look bigger.
  • microscopeA device that makes tiny objects look much bigger.
  • organismA living being such as a plant, animal or microorganism.
  • cellThe basic unit of life, from which living beings are made.
  • cell membraneThe outer layer that encloses the cytoplasm and nucleus of a cell.
  • cytoplasmThe jelly-like part of a cell where most life processes take place.
  • nucleusThe part of a cell that regulates its activities and growth.
  • cell wallAn extra outer covering that gives a plant cell strength.
  • chloroplastA plastid that contains chlorophyll and helps a plant make food.
  • vacuoleA space in a plant cell that stores substances and helps keep its shape.
  • tissueA group of similar cells working together.
  • organA body part made from different tissues working together.
  • microorganismA tiny living being that usually needs a microscope to be seen.
  • multicellularMade from many cells that specialise and cooperate.
  • decompositionThe breakdown of complex dead material into simpler substances.
