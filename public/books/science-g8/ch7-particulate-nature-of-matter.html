Words this chapter explains

  • matterMatter is composed of extremely small particles.
  • physical changeIt changes the size of a substance without forming a new substance.
  • solutionIt forms when sugar dissolves in water.
  • interparticle spaceIt is the space present between particles.
  • interparticle attractionIt is the force that holds particles together.
  • interparticle distanceGreater distance between particles weakens their attractive forces.
  • melting pointIt is the minimum temperature at which a solid melts into a liquid.
  • boiling pointIt is the temperature at which a liquid boils and becomes vapour.
  • evaporationIt is slow vapour formation at the surface of a liquid.
  • fluidLiquids and gases are fluids because they flow and have no fixed shape.
  • insolubleIt cannot dissolve in a particular liquid.
  • thermal energyIt determines the physical state of matter.
  • vapourIt is the gaseous state formed from a liquid.
  • atomIt is one of the tiny particles that make up all matter.
  • moleculeIt is a stable particle formed by joined atoms.
  • ParmanuIt is the name Acharya Kanad gave to an indivisible particle of matter.
