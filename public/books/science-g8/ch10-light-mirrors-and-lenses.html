Words this chapter explains

  • spherical mirrorA curved mirror whose reflecting surface is spherical.
  • concave mirrorA spherical mirror whose reflecting surface curves inwards.
  • convex mirrorA spherical mirror whose reflecting surface curves outwards.
  • plane mirrorA flat mirror that forms an erect image of the same size.
  • erectAn image that is the right way up rather than upside down.
  • invertedAn image that is turned upside down.
  • lateral inversionA mirror-image change seen in images made by all three types of mirror.
  • angle of reflectionThe angle between the normal and the reflected ray.
  • convergingA lens that brings light rays closer together.
  • divergingA lens that makes light rays spread apart.
  • solar concentratorA device that concentrates sunlight into a small area.
