Words this chapter explains

  • ORSIt is a solution used to treat dehydration and keep the body hydrated.
  • Uniform mixtureIt is a mixture whose components are evenly distributed.
  • SoluteIt is the component that dissolves in a solvent.
  • SolventIt is the component in which the solute dissolves.
  • Unsaturated solutionIt is a solution in which more solute can still dissolve.
  • Saturated solutionIt is a solution in which no more solute can dissolve at that temperature.
  • ConcentrationIt tells us the amount of solute in a fixed quantity of solution.
  • Dilute solutionIt is a solution containing a relatively smaller amount of solute.
  • Concentrated solutionIt is a solution containing a relatively larger amount of solute.
  • SolubilityIt is the maximum amount of solute that dissolves in a fixed quantity.
  • DensityIt is the mass present in a unit volume of a substance.
  • MassIt is the quantity of matter present in an object.
  • VolumeIt is the space occupied by an object or substance.
  • MeniscusIt is the curved surface formed by a liquid in a measuring cylinder.
