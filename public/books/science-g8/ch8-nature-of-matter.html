Words this chapter explains

  • mixtureA mixture contains substances that keep their own properties.
  • componentsComponents are the individual substances that make up a mixture.
  • non-uniformA non-uniform mixture has components that can be seen separately.
  • uniformA uniform mixture has components that are evenly mixed and cannot be told apart.
  • alloyAn alloy is a mixture of metals that looks the same throughout.
  • pollutantA pollutant is something such as dust that is not an integral part of air.
  • AQIThe AQI is a tool used to describe air quality.
  • pure substanceA pure substance contains only one type of particle.
  • adulterationAdulteration means illegally adding cheaper or poor-quality substances to a product.
  • elementAn element is a substance that cannot be broken down into simpler substances.
  • compoundA compound forms when elements combine chemically in a fixed ratio.
  • metalloidMetalloids have properties between those of metals and non-metals.
  • mineralMinerals are natural solid substances found on Earth.
