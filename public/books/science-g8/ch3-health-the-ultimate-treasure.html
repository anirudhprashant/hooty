Words this chapter explains

  • dinacharyaA daily routine that helps maintain balance and good health.
  • ritucharyaA routine followed according to the season to support health.
  • prakritiA person’s body constitution, which is considered when choosing suitable food.
  • pranayamaSimple breathing exercises practised to support health.
  • Air Quality IndexAn index that shows how clean the air is.
  • symptomSomething a person feels, such as pain or tiredness.
  • signSomething that can be seen or measured, such as fever.
  • pathogenAn organism that causes disease, such as a bacterium, virus, fungus or worm.
  • communicable diseaseA disease caused by pathogens that can spread between people.
  • non-communicable diseaseA disease that does not spread between people and is linked with lifestyle, diet or environment.
  • vectorAn insect, such as a mosquito or housefly, that spreads pathogens.
  • deficiency diseaseA disease caused by a lack of a particular nutrient in the diet.
  • chronic diseaseA disease that may persist for a long time, often for more than three months.
  • immunityThe natural ability of the body to fight diseases.
  • vaccineA preparation that trains the immune system to recognise and attack harmful germs.
  • antibiotic resistanceA condition in which bacteria survive and multiply despite an antibiotic that once killed them.
