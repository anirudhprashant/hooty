Words this chapter explains

  • curiosityCuriosity is the desire to learn and ask questions about the world.
  • investigationInvestigation means asking focused questions, testing ideas and using observations to improve understanding.
  • observationsObservations are the things we notice and record while investigating a question.
  • experimentAn experiment is a planned test in which changes and results are carefully observed.
  • systematic investigationA systematic investigation is a careful and organised way of asking and testing questions.
  • forcesForces can make objects speed up, slow down or change direction.
  • pressurePressure shows how force is spread over an object.
  • particlesParticles are the tiny pieces from which materials are made.
  • elementsElements are pure substances.
  • compoundsCompounds are made when two or more elements are bonded together.
  • mixturesMixtures are combinations that can be separated physically.
  • solutionsA solution can form when sugar dissolves in tea.
  • light raysLight rays can reflect from surfaces and bend when passing through lenses.
  • atmosphereThe atmosphere provides Earth with the oxygen we breathe.
  • ultraviolet raysUltraviolet rays are harmful rays that the atmosphere shields us from.
  • climate patternsClimate patterns are the Earth’s patterns that can be disrupted by small temperature changes.
