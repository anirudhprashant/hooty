Words this chapter explains

  • greenhouse effectThe greenhouse effect happens when atmospheric gases trap heat.
  • radiationRadiation is the energy given off by a warmed body.
  • habitable zoneThe habitable zone is the range around a star where liquid water can exist.
  • ozone layerThe ozone layer is the part of the atmosphere that blocks harmful ultraviolet rays.
  • hydrosphereThe hydrosphere consists of all the water on Earth.
  • geosphereThe geosphere consists of Earth’s rocks, soils and minerals.
  • geodiversityGeodiversity is the variety of Earth’s landforms, rocks and soils.
  • biosphereThe biosphere includes all living beings and the places where they live.
  • reproductionReproduction makes new organisms so that life continues.
  • genesGenes are instructions stored inside every living cell.
  • gametesGametes are special cells that carry half a parent’s genetic material.
  • asexual reproductionAsexual reproduction uses one parent to produce identical offspring.
  • sexual reproductionSexual reproduction combines genetic instructions from two parents.
