Words this chapter explains

  • habitatA habitat is the place where an organism lives and gets the conditions it needs.
  • biotic componentsBiotic components are the living beings found in a habitat.
  • abiotic componentsAbiotic components are the non-living things found in a habitat.
  • populationA population is a group of the same type of organism in a habitat at a given time.
  • communityA community is made up of different populations sharing the same habitat.
  • pollinationPollination is the movement of pollen from stamens to carpels.
  • ecosystemAn ecosystem forms when living and non-living parts interact in an area.
  • autotrophAn autotroph makes its own food, as plants do.
  • heterotrophA heterotroph depends on other organisms for food.
  • food chainA food chain is a sequence showing who eats whom in an ecosystem.
  • trophic levelA trophic level is the position of an organism in a food chain.
  • food webA food web is a network formed by interlinked food chains.
  • mutualismIn mutualism, both living organisms gain a benefit.
  • commensalismIn commensalism, one organism benefits while the other is unaffected.
  • parasitismIn parasitism, one organism benefits while the other is harmed.
