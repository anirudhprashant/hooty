Words this chapter explains

  • forceA push or pull applied to an object.
  • interactionWhen two objects act on one another.
  • contact forceA force that acts only when two objects physically touch.
  • muscular forceForce produced by the action of muscles.
  • frictionA force that acts between touching surfaces and opposes movement.
  • non-contact forceA force whose effect can be experienced without touching.
  • magnetic forceA push or pull exerted by a magnet.
  • static chargeAn electrical charge that does not move by itself.
  • charged objectAn object that has acquired static charges.
  • electrostatic forceForce exerted by a charged body on another charged or uncharged body.
  • gravitational forceThe Earth's pull on objects towards itself.
  • weightThe force with which the Earth pulls an object towards itself.
  • spring balanceA device that measures weight using the stretching of a spring.
  • upthrustThe upward force applied by a liquid to an object.
  • Archimedes’ PrincipleAn immersed object experiences an upward force equal to the weight of the liquid it displaces.
