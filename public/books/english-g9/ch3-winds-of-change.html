Words this chapter explains

  • indigenousIt means local or coming from the place where it originated.
  • innovativeIt means new and original in its approach.
  • invokedIt means brought out a feeling or thought.
  • initiativesThey are actions taken to improve a situation.
  • pankhaIt is a general word for fans, and ‘pankh’ means a bird’s feather.
  • pankhiIt means a small plumed fan used in ancient India.
  • encrustedIt means covered or decorated with something.
  • solaIt is a type of water grass used to make fans.
  • antiquityIt means the distant past.
  • allegoryIt is a story or poem that expresses a larger idea indirectly.
  • metaphorIt is a comparison that does not use ‘like’ or ‘as’.
  • collocationIt is a usual combination of words.
