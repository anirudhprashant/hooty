Words this chapter explains

  • microbesVery tiny living things that can grow on food and change it.
  • microscopeAn instrument used to see very tiny things.
  • mouldA type of tiny living growth that makes coloured patches on spoiled food.
  • colonyA large group of tiny living things living together.
  • moistureTiny water or wetness in something.
  • temperatureHow hot or cold something is.
  • preservationKeeping food safe and unspoiled for a long time.
  • dehydrationRemoving water or moisture from food so microbes cannot grow.
  • picklingPreserving food in oil, salt or spices so it lasts longer.
  • refrigerationKeeping food cold in a fridge to slow down microbes.
  • freezingMaking food very cold so microbes slow down.
  • matkaA clay pot used to keep water cool.
  • air tightClosed so that air cannot go in or out.
  • digestionThe process by which the body breaks down food.
  • indigestionA stomach problem when food is not digested well.
  • salivaLiquid in the mouth that helps digest food.
