Words this chapter explains

  • freshwaterWater that is not salty and is used for drinking and farming.
  • saltwaterWater that has salt in it, like ocean water.
  • water vapourWater in gas form, like steam.
  • water cycleThe continuous circular movement of water in nature.
  • groundwaterWater stored deep underground in soil and rocks.
  • rechargeTo refill groundwater by letting water seep into the ground.
  • surface waterWater found on the surface in rivers, ponds, and lakes.
  • soak pitsPits that help rainwater seep into the ground.
  • watershed managementCaring for land and water so rainwater is stored and used well.
  • rainwater harvestingCollecting and storing rainwater for use.
  • aquaticLiving or growing in water.
  • habitatThe natural home of plants and animals.
  • phumdisFloating islands in a Manipur lake.
  • water-positive villageA village that manages water so it has enough or more.
  • salt pansFlat areas where seawater is dried to make salt.
  • endangeredAt risk of disappearing from the world.
