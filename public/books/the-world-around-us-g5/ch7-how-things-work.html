Words this chapter explains

  • energySomething that makes things move, light up, produce sound, do work and change temperature.
  • fuelMaterial like petrol, diesel, wood, coal or cooking gas that gives energy when burned.
  • electricityA form of energy used to run machines and give movement, sound, light and heat.
  • clean energyEnergy from natural sources like Sun, wind or water that does not pollute.
  • energy efficiencyUsing less energy to do the same work.
  • solar panelsPanels that capture sunlight and turn it into electricity.
  • windmillsMachines that spin with wind to make electricity.
  • watermillsWater-powered mills that use flowing water to grind grains.
  • pollutionHarmful smoke or waste that makes air or environment dirty.
  • sound energyEnergy produced when things vibrate and make sounds.
  • heat energyEnergy that makes things warm, like sunlight warming water.
  • stored energyEnergy kept in the body or batteries for later use.
  • batteriesThings that contain stored energy.
  • Vaastu ShastraAncient Indian system of design that plans buildings in harmony with nature.
  • solar energyEnergy from the Sun.
