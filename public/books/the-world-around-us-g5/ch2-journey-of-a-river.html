Words this chapter explains

  • tributariesSmall streams that join a bigger river.
  • perennial riverA river that flows all year round.
  • seasonal riversRivers that flow only when they receive water in the rainy season.
  • deltaMany small streams formed where a river spreads out at the sea.
  • Dakshina GangaA name for Godavari meaning Ganga of the south.
  • Godavari PushkaramA holy event when people take a dip in Godavari waters.
  • Coringa Mangrove ForestsThe place where Godavari's journey ends at the sea.
  • ecotourismVisiting natural places to enjoy beauty and learn about nature without harming it.
  • damA giant wall that blocks a river's flow and stores water.
  • reservoirA large water body where a dam stores water.
  • fish migrationWhen fish travel from the sea into rivers to lay eggs.
  • water ATMsSpecial machines that give clean water when you put in a card or coin.
  • National Water MissionA programme that works to manage water properly across India.
  • eco-friendly productsProducts that create less waste and are less harmful to nature.
  • fertilisersMaterials used to help crops grow, which can wash into rivers.
  • floodWhen a river overflows its banks and covers land.
