Words this chapter explains

  • livingSomething that has the characteristics of life, such as growing, breathing and reproducing.
  • non-livingSomething that does not show the essential characteristics of life.
  • movementA change in position or body shape, such as a flower opening or a leaf folding.
  • nutritionThe taking in of food needed for growth and development.
  • respirationThe process in which living beings take in air and release air.
  • excretionThe removal of waste products from the body.
  • stimulusAnything or any event that makes a living being respond.
  • reproductionThe production of new living beings of one's own kind.
  • stomataTiny pores on leaves that let plants take air in and out.
  • germinationThe growth of a seed into a sprout or young plant.
  • seed coatThe outer covering that protects a seed.
  • life cycleThe stages through which a living being passes from one stage to the next.
  • larvaThe worm-like young stage of a mosquito or silk moth.
  • pupaThe stage in which a mosquito or silk moth changes into its adult form.
  • frogletA young frog that has legs but still has a tail.
