Words this chapter explains

  • magnetic compassA device with a magnetised needle that shows the north-south direction.
  • lodestoneA magnet that occurs naturally in the environment.
  • artificial magnetA magnet made by people rather than found naturally.
  • magnetic materialA material that is attracted towards a magnet.
  • iron filingsVery small pieces of iron used to show where a magnet attracts iron.
  • North poleThe end of a magnet that points towards north when it can move freely.
  • South poleThe end of a magnet that points towards south when it can move freely.
  • North-seeking poleThe name given to the end of a magnet that points north.
  • South-seeking poleThe name given to the end of a magnet that points south.
  • compass needleA small magnetised needle that can turn and show north and south.
  • magnetisedMade able to attract iron or other magnetic materials.
  • attractionThe pulling together of unlike poles of two magnets.
  • repulsionThe pushing apart of like poles of two magnets.
