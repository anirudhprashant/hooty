Words this chapter explains

  • handpickingRemoving unwanted pieces from a mixture by picking them out with the fingers.
  • threshingBeating stalks so that the grains separate from them.
  • winnowingSeparating heavier and lighter parts of a mixture using wind or blown air.
  • soopA bamboo tray used to separate heavier and lighter components.
  • thresherA machine that separates grains from stalks and husk.
  • sievingSeparating solid particles of different sizes by using a sieve.
  • mixtureTwo or more substances mixed together.
  • solutionA mixture formed when a substance such as salt dissolves in a liquid.
  • sedimentationThe settling of a heavier insoluble component at the bottom of a liquid.
  • decantationRemoving liquid from a vessel after a settled solid has formed at the bottom.
  • filtrationPassing a liquid through a filter to separate insoluble solids from it.
  • filtrateThe clear liquid that has passed through a filter.
  • residueThe solid material left behind on a filter.
  • churningShaking or beating curd so that butter separates from it.
  • magnetic separationSeparating magnetic and non-magnetic substances with a magnet.
