Words this chapter explains

  • wholesomeGood for the body and suitable for eating.
  • culinary practicesThe methods and habits used to prepare food.
  • carbohydrateA food component that gives the body energy.
  • fatA food component that stores energy in the body.
  • proteinA body-building nutrient that supports growth and repair.
  • nutrientA useful substance in food that helps the body function.
  • deficiency diseasesIllnesses caused by not getting enough nutrients for a long time.
  • scurvyA disease caused by a lack of vitamin C.
  • goitreA disorder that causes swelling at the front of the neck.
  • roughageThe part of food that helps move undigested food through the body.
  • vitaminsNutrients that protect the body and keep it healthy.
  • fortified foodsFoods with extra nutrients added during processing.
  • junk foodsUnhealthy foods that contain lots of sugar and fat but few nutrients.
  • food milesThe distance travelled by food from its place of production to the consumer.
  • iodised saltCommon salt mixed with the required quantity of iodine salts.
  • milletsSmall, nutritious grains that can be grown in many climatic conditions.
