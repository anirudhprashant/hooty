Words this chapter explains

  • BiodiversityThe variety of living plants and animals found in a place or region.
  • VenationThe pattern in which veins are arranged in a leaf.
  • Reticulate venationA net-like pattern of veins found in a leaf.
  • Parallel venationA leaf-vein pattern in which the veins run side by side.
  • TaprootThe main root of a plant, with smaller side roots growing from it.
  • Fibrous rootA bunch of similar-sized thin roots growing from the base of a stem.
  • CotyledonOne of the parts into which a seed divides.
  • DicotyledonA plant whose seed has two cotyledons.
  • MonocotyledonA plant whose seed has a single cotyledon.
  • AdaptationA special feature that helps a plant or animal survive in a particular region.
  • HabitatThe place where a plant or animal lives.
  • TerrestrialRelating to plants or animals that live on land.
  • AquaticRelating to plants or animals that live in water.
  • AmphibianAn animal that can live both in water and on land.
  • Sacred grovesUndisturbed patches of forest protected by local communities.
