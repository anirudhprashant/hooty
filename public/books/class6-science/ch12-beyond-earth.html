Words this chapter explains

  • ConstellationA named region of the sky that often includes a pattern of stars.
  • NakshatraIn Indian astronomy, this can mean one star or a group of stars.
  • PolarisThe nearly stationary star in the north that helps people find the North direction.
  • Ursa MajorThe northern constellation containing the Big Dipper.
  • Light pollutionExcessive artificial light at night that makes the night sky harder to see.
  • Astronomical unitA distance unit equal to about the distance from the Sun to Earth.
  • RevolutionThe movement of an object around the Sun.
  • SatelliteAn object that moves around a much larger object, such as a planet.
  • CraterA round, bowl-shaped hollow on the Moon’s surface.
  • AsteroidA small rocky object in the Solar System with an irregular shape.
  • CometAn icy-rocky object with a long tail that travels near the Sun.
  • Milky Way GalaxyThe galaxy that contains our Solar System.
  • GalaxyA group containing millions to billions of stars.
  • ExoplanetA planet discovered revolving around another star in our galaxy.
  • TelescopeAn instrument that makes distant sky objects look brighter and larger.
  • OrionA constellation often pictured as a hunter with three stars forming a belt.
