  • metreThe SI unit for measuring length.
  • centimetreOne of the 100 equal divisions in a metre.
  • millimetreOne of the ten equal parts of a centimetre.
  • kilometreA larger unit of length equal to 1000 metres.
  • unitThe named part of a measurement, such as handspan or metre.
  • flexible measuring tapeA bendable tape used when a straight scale is not suitable.
  • reference pointA fixed object or point from which a distance is stated.
  • linear motionMotion in which an object moves along a straight line.
  • circular motionMotion in which an object moves along a circular path.
  • oscillatory motionMotion in which an object moves to and fro about a fixed position.
