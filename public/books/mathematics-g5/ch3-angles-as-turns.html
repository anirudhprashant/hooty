Words this chapter explains

  • full turnA full turn brings a moving part back to where it began.
  • half turnA half turn is half of a complete turn.
  • quarter turnA quarter turn is one equal part of a full turn.
  • right angleA right angle is made by a quarter turn.
  • acute angleAn acute angle is smaller than a right angle.
  • obtuse angleAn obtuse angle is wider than a right angle but smaller than a straight angle.
  • straight angleA straight angle is made when the turn forms a straight line.
  • angleAn angle shows the size of a turn.
  • initial positionThe initial position is where a moving part began.
  • starting pointA starting point marks where a turn begins.
  • clockwise movementClockwise movement is the named direction in which clock hands move.
  • anti-clockwise movementAnti-clockwise movement is opposite to clockwise movement.
  • angle measuring toolAn angle-measuring tool helps us measure turns.
