  • fractionA fraction shows a part of a whole.

  • equal partsEqual parts are pieces of the same size.

  • equivalentEquivalent fractions show the same part of a whole.
  • gridA grid is used to show parts that have been shaded.
  • fraction kitA fraction kit contains pieces used to make wholes.
  • fraction piecesFraction pieces are used to make a whole.

  • halfA half is one of two equal parts of a whole.

  • less thanLess than means that the first amount is smaller.
