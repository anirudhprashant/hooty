Words this chapter explains

  • tessellateCopies of a shape tessellate when they cover a region without gaps or overlaps.
  • regular shapeA shape whose sides are all equal is called a regular shape.
  • equilateral triangleA triangle with all three sides equal is called an equilateral triangle.
  • octagonAn octagon is a shape with eight equal sides.
  • rhombusA rhombus is a shape with all equal sides.
  • isosceles triangleAn isosceles triangle has two equal sides and two equal angles.
  • scalene triangleA scalene triangle has no equal sides.
  • quadrilateralA quadrilateral is a shape with four sides.
  • kiteA kite has two pairs of equal adjacent sides.
  • adjacent sidesAdjacent sides are sides that meet at the same corner.
  • parallelogramA parallelogram has equal pairs of opposite sides.
  • rectangleA rectangle is a parallelogram with four right angles.
  • diameterA diameter is a line drawn across a circle through its centre.
  • icosahedronThe name of this solid tells its number of faces after they are counted.
  • dodecahedronThe name of this solid tells its number of faces after they are counted.
  • vertexA vertex is a point where faces meet.
