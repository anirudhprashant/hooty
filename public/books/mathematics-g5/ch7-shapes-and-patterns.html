  • octagonAn octagon is a shape with eight equal sides.
  • rhombusA rhombus is a shape with all equal sides.
  • isosceles triangleAn isosceles triangle has two equal sides and two equal angles.
  • scalene triangleA scalene triangle has no equal sides.
  • quadrilateralA quadrilateral is a shape with four sides.
  • kiteA kite has two pairs of equal adjacent sides.
  • adjacent sidesAdjacent sides are sides that meet at the same corner.
  • parallelogramA parallelogram has equal pairs of opposite sides.
  • rectangleA rectangle is a parallelogram with four right angles.
