Words this chapter explains

  • fair coinA fair coin is symmetrical and does not favour one side over the other.
  • unbiasedUnbiased means that there is no reason for one side or outcome to occur more often.
  • random tossA random toss allows the object to fall freely without bias or interference.
  • equally likely outcomesEqually likely outcomes have the same chance of occurring.
  • eventAn event is something that can happen in an experiment and has a probability.
  • favourable outcomeA favourable outcome is one that matches the event being considered.
  • empirical probabilityEmpirical probability is based on what happened in repeated trials.
  • theoretical probabilityTheoretical probability uses favourable outcomes and all possible equally likely outcomes.
  • elementary eventAn elementary event has only one outcome of the experiment.
  • complementary eventsComplementary events are an event and the event representing not E.
  • complementThe complement of E represents not E and has probability 1 minus P(E).
  • impossible eventAn impossible event cannot occur and has probability 0.
  • sure eventA sure event must occur and has probability 1.
  • face cardsThe king, queen and jack cards are called face cards.
  • finite outcomesFinite outcomes are possible outcomes that can be listed as a finite number.
  • infinite outcomesInfinite outcomes are possible outcomes that cannot be counted as a finite list.
