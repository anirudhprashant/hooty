Words this chapter explains

  • congruentTwo figures are congruent when they have the same shape and size.
  • similar figuresThese figures have the same shape but may have different sizes.
  • similaritySimilarity is the matching of shapes, angles and side proportions.
  • indirect measurementIndirect measurement finds a height or distance without measuring it directly.
  • scale factorThe scale factor is the common ratio between corresponding sides.
  • Representative FractionThe Representative Fraction is another name for the scale factor.
  • corresponding verticesThese are matching vertices that play the same part in each figure.
  • equiangular trianglesEquiangular triangles have equal corresponding angles.
  • Basic Proportionality TheoremIt says that a line parallel to one side divides the other two sides in the same ratio.
  • converseA converse reverses a theorem by using its conclusion to find its condition.
  • proportionProportion means that corresponding quantities are in the same ratio.
  • AAA similarity criterionThe AAA criterion proves similarity from equal corresponding angles.
  • AA similarity criterionThe AA criterion proves similarity from two pairs of equal angles.
  • SSS similarity criterionThe SSS criterion proves similarity from proportional corresponding sides.
  • SAS similarity criterionThe SAS criterion uses one equal angle and proportional sides around it.
  • RHS Similarity CriterionThis criterion applies to right triangles with proportional hypotenuses and one other side each.
