Words this chapter explains

  • circleA closed shape whose points are all the same distance from its centre.
  • radiusThe distance from the centre of a circle to any point on it.
  • centreThe fixed point from which every point on a circle is equally distant.
  • chordA line segment whose two ends lie on a circle.
  • non-intersecting lineA line that has no common point with a circle.
  • point of contactThe one point where a tangent meets a circle.
  • normalThe line containing the radius through the point of contact.
  • external pointA point outside a circle from which tangents can be drawn.
  • parallel tangentsTwo tangents that never meet because they run in the same direction.
  • concentric circlesTwo circles with the same centre but different radii.
  • angle bisectorA line that divides an angle into two equal angles.
  • isosceles triangleA triangle with two equal sides.
