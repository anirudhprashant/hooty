Words this chapter explains

  • trigonometryThe study of relationships between the sides and angles of a triangle.
  • trigonometric ratioA ratio involving the sides of a right triangle and one of its acute angles.
  • hypotenuseThe side opposite the right angle in a right-angled triangle.
  • opposite sideThe side that lies across from the angle being considered.
  • adjacent sideA side next to the angle, other than the hypotenuse.
  • sineThe ratio of the opposite side to the hypotenuse.
  • cosineThe ratio of the adjacent side to the hypotenuse.
  • tangentThe ratio of the opposite side to the adjacent side.
  • cosecantThe reciprocal trigonometric ratio of sine.
  • secantThe reciprocal trigonometric ratio of cosine.
  • cotangentThe reciprocal trigonometric ratio of tangent.
  • reciprocalThe opposite ratio in a reciprocal pair such as sine and cosecant.
  • equilateral triangleA triangle whose three angles are each 60° in this chapter.
  • trigonometric identityAn equation involving trigonometric ratios that is true for all the angles concerned.
