Words this chapter explains

  • combination of solidsA shape made by joining two or more basic solids.
  • total surface areaThe whole outside area of a solid, including every exposed part.
  • curved surface areaThe outside area of a solid that does not include its flat circular faces.
  • hemisphereA solid that forms half of a sphere.
  • coneA basic solid with a circular base and a pointed top.
  • cylinderA basic solid with two equal circular bases and a straight curved surface.
  • base radiusThe radius of a solid's circular base.
  • slant heightThe sloping height of a cone from its tip to its base edge.
  • volumeThe space occupied by a solid.
  • apparent capacityThe capacity a container appears to have from its usual cylinder shape.
  • actual capacityThe real amount a container can hold after any raised or scooped part is considered.
  • hemispherical depressionA half-sphere-shaped part scooped out of a solid.
  • circumscribesSurrounds a solid closely, as the cylinder surrounds the toy.
  • hemispherical partThe half-sphere section attached to another solid.
