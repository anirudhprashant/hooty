Words this chapter explains

  • real numbersNumbers that include both rational and irrational numbers.
  • irrational numberA number that cannot be written as p divided by q with integers p and q and q not equal to zero.
  • Euclid’s division algorithmA rule for dividing one positive integer by another with a remainder smaller than the divisor.
  • divisibilityThe property of one integer dividing exactly into another integer.
  • remainderThe part left after a division, which is smaller than the divisor.
  • Fundamental Theorem of ArithmeticEvery composite number has a unique prime factorisation apart from the order of its factors.
  • composite numberA number that can be expressed as a product of primes.
  • prime numberA prime factor used to build a natural number through multiplication.
  • prime factorisationWriting a number as a product of its prime factors.
  • HCFThe common factor used when finding the greatest shared factor of positive integers.
  • LCMThe common multiple used when finding the least shared multiple of positive integers.
  • coprimeTwo integers are coprime when they have no common factor other than 1.
  • proof by contradictionA proof that starts by assuming the opposite of what must be shown.
  • factor treeA diagram used to break a number into factors and then prime factors.
  • ascending orderAn arrangement from the smallest value to the largest value.
