Words this chapter explains

  • trigonometric ratiosRatios used to relate angles and sides in right-angled triangles.
  • line of sightThe line joining an observer’s eye to the point being viewed.
  • angle of elevationThe angle between the line of sight and the horizontal when the point is above.
  • angle of depressionThe angle between the line of sight and the horizontal when the point is below.
  • horizontal levelThe level of a flat horizontal line used for comparison.
  • right-angled triangleA triangle containing a 90-degree angle, such as triangle ABC.
  • ladderThe sloping side BC used by the electrician to reach the pole.
  • shadowThe length BC cast on the ground by the tower.
  • Sun’s altitudeThe angle at which the Sun is above the horizontal level.
  • transversalThe line PB that crosses the parallel lines PQ and BD.
  • alternate anglesAngles on opposite sides of a transversal that are equal.
  • widthThe distance AB across the river between its two banks.
  • flagstaffThe pole BD on which the flag is hoisted.
