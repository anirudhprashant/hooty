Words this chapter explains

  • quadratic equationAn equation that can be written as ax2 + bx + c = 0.
  • standard formThe usual arrangement ax2 + bx + c = 0.
  • rootA value of x that makes the quadratic equation true.
  • factorisationSplitting a polynomial into a product of factors.
  • linear factorA factor that can be set equal to zero to find a root.
  • discriminantThe expression b2 – 4ac used to determine the nature of the roots.
  • distinct real rootsTwo different real values that satisfy the equation.
  • equal real rootsTwo equal real values that both satisfy the equation.
  • coincident rootsEqual roots that meet at the same point.
  • no real rootsRoots that are not real numbers, so there is no real value of x.
  • quadratic formulaThe formula used to calculate the roots of a quadratic equation.
  • real numberA number that may be used as a coefficient such as a, b or c.
