Words this chapter explains

  • variableThe symbol used in a polynomial, such as x or y.
  • degreeThe highest power of the variable in a polynomial.
  • linear polynomialA polynomial whose degree is one.
  • quadratic polynomialA polynomial whose degree is two.
  • cubic polynomialA polynomial whose degree is three.
  • valueThe result obtained by replacing x with a chosen number.
  • coefficientA number that multiplies a power of the variable.
  • graphA drawing of y = p(x) used to find its zeroes.
  • x-axisThe axis where a graph shows the x-coordinate of a zero.
  • x-coordinateThe number on the x-axis for a point on a graph.
  • parabolaThe curve that is the graph of a quadratic polynomial.
  • factoriseTo rewrite a polynomial as a product of factors.
  • factorAn expression that multiplies another expression in a product.
  • at mostNo more than the stated number.
  • constant termThe term in a polynomial that has no variable.
