Words this chapter explains

  • termOne of the numbers appearing in a list or progression.
  • common differenceThe fixed number added to each preceding term to obtain the next term of an AP.
  • fixed numberA number that stays the same when used to obtain each succeeding term.
  • finite APAn arithmetic progression with a limited number of terms and a last term.
  • infinite APAn arithmetic progression that continues without having a last term.
  • last termThe final term of a finite arithmetic progression.
  • nth termThe term at position n in an arithmetic progression.
  • arithmetic meanThe middle value of two numbers a and c when a, b and c form an AP.
  • patternA repeated arrangement or rule that connects the terms in a list.
  • APsShort names used for lists whose consecutive terms have equal differences.
