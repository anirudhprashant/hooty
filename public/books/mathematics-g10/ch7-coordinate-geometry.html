Words this chapter explains

  • coordinate axesA pair of perpendicular axes used to locate a point on a plane.
  • y-coordinateThe distance of a point from the x-axis.
  • straight lineThe graph obtained by representing a linear equation graphically.
  • Pythagoras theoremA theorem used to calculate distances in the given right triangles.
  • distance formulaA rule for finding the distance between two points from their coordinates.
  • perpendicular bisectorThe line on which points equidistant from A and B lie.
  • section formulaA rule for finding the coordinates of an internal division point.
  • internal divisionDivision in which the point lies between the two endpoints.
  • external divisionDivision in which the point lies on the line but outside the segment.
  • collinearLying on the same straight line.
  • equidistantHaving equal distances from two points.
  • trisectionDivision into three equal parts.
