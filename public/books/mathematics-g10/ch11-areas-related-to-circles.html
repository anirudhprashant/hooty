Words this chapter explains

  • sectorA sector is a part of a circle enclosed by two radii and an arc.
  • segmentA segment is a part of a circle enclosed between a chord and an arc.
  • arcAn arc is a curved part of a circle between two points.
  • angle of the sectorIt is the angle formed by the two radii of a sector.
  • angle at the centreIt is the angle made at the centre of a circle.
  • minor sectorA minor sector is the smaller sector formed by two radii and an arc.
  • major sectorA major sector is the larger sector formed by two radii and an arc.
  • minor segmentA minor segment is the smaller region between a chord and its arc.
  • major segmentA major segment is the larger region between a chord and its arc.
  • discA disc is the whole region inside a circle.
  • Unitary MethodThis method finds a part by comparing it with the whole.
  • circumferenceThe circumference is the distance around a circle.
