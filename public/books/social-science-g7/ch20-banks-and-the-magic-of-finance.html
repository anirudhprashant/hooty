Words this chapter explains

  • Financial infrastructureIt is a network that enables financial transactions and helps people manage money.
  • DepositIt is money placed in a bank account under the bank’s withdrawal terms.
  • InterestIt is the amount charged for borrowing or gained by lending money.
  • CompoundingIt is earning interest on interest gained in earlier years.
  • PassbookIt is a bank document that records deposits, withdrawals and other transactions.
  • LoanIt is borrowed money that must be repaid later with interest.
  • DebitIt means taking money out of an account.
  • CreditIt means receiving money in an account.
  • Central bankIt supervises a country’s banking system and manages related policies.
  • Payment systemIt clears and settles transactions so that funds can be transferred.
  • PINIt is a short numeric code used for security in financial transactions.
  • OTPIt is a temporary code used to verify identity or authorise a transaction.
  • ShareIt is a unit of ownership in a company.
  • Stock exchangeIt is a marketplace where financial securities such as stocks are traded.
