Words this chapter explains

  • stateA political organisation with people, territory, government and sovereignty.
  • governmentA group or system that runs the country and manages its work.
  • sovereigntyIt means that a state controls its own affairs without outside interference.
  • territoryIt is the geographical area of a state with clear boundaries.
  • bureaucracyIt is the permanent group of officers that helps the government run smoothly.
  • civil servantsThese are unelected government officers who implement laws and manage public services.
  • grievance redressalIt is a system for registering complaints and getting them addressed.
  • transparencyIt means giving citizens clear information about government actions and public spending.
  • civil societyThese are groups of citizens who raise issues and work with the government.
  • decentralisationIt means spreading power and decision-making across different levels of government.
