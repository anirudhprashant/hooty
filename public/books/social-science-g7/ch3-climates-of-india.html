Words this chapter explains

  • climateThe long-term pattern of weather in a region over several decades.
  • seasonA recurring part of the year linked with the Earth's movement around the Sun.
  • monsoonSeasonal winds over the Indian Ocean region that also bring seasonal rain.
  • latitudeA measure of distance from the Equator.
  • topographyThe physical surface features of an area, such as mountains, hills, valleys, slopes and coasts.
  • microclimateA small area whose climate differs from the surrounding region.
  • cycloneA rotating storm that may form around an intense low-pressure system.
  • floodAn overflow of water into normally dry land.
  • landslideThe sudden collapse of rock, soil or debris.
  • glacial burstA sudden break of water through a barrier of rocks and ice.
  • climate changeSignificant long-term changes in climate, temperature, precipitation or weather events.
  • fossil fuelsEnergy sources formed from the buried remains of ancient plants and animals.
  • mitigationSteps taken to slow global warming and reduce the causes of climate change.
