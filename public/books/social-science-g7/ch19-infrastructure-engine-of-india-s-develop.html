Words this chapter explains

  • physical infrastructureTangible structures and networks that keep cities and villages working.
  • utilitiesServices such as electricity and water that people need.
  • communication networksSystems that carry messages, images and videos through cables, towers and satellites.
  • irrigationSupplying water to fields so that crops can grow.
  • expresswayA fast road that joins cities across different states.
  • tunnelA passage built through or under difficult terrain.
  • indigeneousPeople whose local knowledge and skills help shape living root bridges.
  • railway networkThe connected system of railway tracks and stations across India.
  • cargoGoods that are carried by trains, ships or aircraft.
  • TEUA unit used to measure container-ship capacity and port activity.
  • e-governanceThe use of communication technology by government to provide services to citizens.
  • e-commerceBuying or selling products and services through online platforms or the internet.
  • patentA right granted to be the only person or company allowed to use an invention for a set number of years.
  • social infrastructurePublic facilities such as schools, hospitals, parks and libraries that support communities.
  • collective responsibilityThe duty of everyone to look after public infrastructure.
  • sustainable infrastructureInfrastructure that uses cleaner energy and materials to reduce environmental harm.
