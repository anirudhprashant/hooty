Words this chapter explains

  • UrbanisationThe growth of towns and cities, where people live and work closely together.
  • JanapadaA territory where a clan of people has settled down under a rājā.
  • MahājanapadaA bigger state formed when several early janapadas merged together.
  • RājāA king or ruler who leads a janapada.
  • SabhāAn assembly or council where matters of the clan are discussed.
  • GaṇaA state where the assembly chose the ruler and made major decisions together.
  • MonarchyA system where one rājā is the final authority.
  • HereditaryPassed on from a father to his son in the same family.
  • MoatA deep, wide ditch full of water around a fort or city.
  • FortificationsStrong walls and defences built to protect a capital or city.
  • MetallurgyThe skill of extracting metal from ore and shaping it into tools or weapons.
  • Punch-marked coinsEarly silver coins with symbols pressed into them.
  • VarṇaOne of the four broad social groups that came from Vedic texts.
  • JātiA community of people linked to one professional occupation.
  • DakṣhiṇapathaA major trade and travel route that went south from Kauśhāmbī.
  • UttarapathaA major trade and travel route that connected the northwest to the Ganga plains.
