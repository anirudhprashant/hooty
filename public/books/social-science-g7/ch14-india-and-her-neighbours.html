Words this chapter explains

  • regionalismCooperation among countries for the benefit of the whole region.
  • PartitionThe 1947 separation that left Pakistan no longer part of India.
  • gurdwaraA sacred Sikh site visited by worshippers and pilgrims.
  • transboundary river systemA river system shared by two or more countries.
  • open borderA border that people can cross without a passport or visa.
  • landlockedA country without a sea coast.
  • DrukyulThe name used by Bhutan’s inhabitants for their country.
  • hydroelectric powerElectricity produced using the power of flowing water.
