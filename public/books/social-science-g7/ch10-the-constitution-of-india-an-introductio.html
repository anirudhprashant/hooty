Words this chapter explains

  • DemocracyA system of government where citizens have equal political rights and can hold rulers accountable.
  • JudiciaryThe branch of government that interprets laws, decides whether they are broken and gives suitable punishment.
  • Checks and balancesControls that keep the three organs of government fair, responsible and accountable.
  • Constituent AssemblyThe group of representatives that developed and wrote the Constitution of India.
  • Civilisational heritageThe traditions, values and ideas passed down through Indian societies over many years.
  • Fundamental RightsRights promised to citizens that the government and courts must protect.
  • Directive Principles of State PolicyGuidelines that set out social and economic goals for the government to achieve.
  • Fundamental DutiesDuties that citizens must follow towards the Constitution and the country.
  • AmendmentAn official change made to the Constitution.
  • PreambleThe opening statement that gives the essence of the Constitution’s guiding values.
  • SecularMeans that citizens may follow any religion and the country has no official religion.
  • Panchayati Raj SystemIndia’s system of local government, which was added to the Constitution in 1992.
