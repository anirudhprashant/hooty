Words this chapter explains

  • weatherWeather is the condition of the atmosphere at a particular time and place.
  • troposphereThe troposphere is the atmospheric layer closest to the Earth's surface.
  • temperatureTemperature tells us how hot or cold the atmosphere is.
  • atmospheric pressureAtmospheric pressure is the pressure made by the weight of the air above and around us.
  • windWind is the movement of air, including its speed and direction.
  • precipitationPrecipitation is water such as rain, snow, sleet or hail falling from the sky.
  • humidityHumidity is the amount of water vapour present in the air.
  • water vapourWater vapour is water in gaseous rather than liquid form.
  • forecastA forecast is a prediction of what the weather will do.
  • meteorologyMeteorology is the systematic study of weather and its evolution.
  • barometerA barometer is an instrument used to measure atmospheric pressure.
  • depressionA depression is a low-pressure system that can develop into a storm or cyclone.
  • wind vaneA wind vane has a pointer that shows the direction of the wind.
  • hygrometerA hygrometer is an instrument used to measure humidity.
