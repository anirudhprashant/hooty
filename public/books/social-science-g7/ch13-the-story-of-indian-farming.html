Words this chapter explains

  • piscicultureRearing fish as part of farming.
  • animal husbandryRaising animals such as cattle, sheep and goats.
  • agroclimatic zoneAn area grouped by climate, soil, terrain and vegetation to guide farming.
  • kharifThe cropping season for crops grown during the monsoon.
  • rabiThe cropping season for crops grown during winter.
  • zaidThe cropping season for crops grown during summer.
  • humusDecayed plant and animal matter that adds nutrients and holds moisture in soil.
  • weatheringThe breaking down of rocks and minerals into soil particles.
  • siltFine sediment made of rock and mineral particles between sand and clay.
  • alluvial soilFertile soil formed from deposits carried and laid down by rivers.
  • soil conservationManaging soil so that it does not lose its productivity.
  • crop rotationGrowing different crops in the same field during different seasons.
  • terrace farmingFarming on flat steps cut into a hillside.
  • drip irrigationDelivering water slowly and directly to plant roots.
  • hectareA unit of land area equal to 10,000 square metres.
