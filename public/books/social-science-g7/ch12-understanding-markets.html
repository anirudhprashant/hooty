Words this chapter explains

  • NeedA need is something a person must have to survive, such as food and water.
  • WantA want is something a person would like to have but can live without.
  • MarketA market is a place where people buy and sell goods.
  • TradeTrade is the buying, selling or exchange of goods and services.
  • PricePrice is the amount at which a buyer and seller agree to trade.
  • Inputs for productionInputs for production are materials or resources used to make goods and services.
  • ManufacturerA manufacturer makes goods that are sold in markets.
  • DemandDemand is the amount of a product that consumers can and want to buy.
  • SupplySupply is the amount of a product that sellers can and want to sell.
  • ExportExport means selling goods or services to a buyer in another country.
  • ImportImport means buying goods or services from another country.
  • Cold storageCold storage keeps perishable goods at low temperatures so they remain preserved.
  • AggregatorAn aggregator combines offers from sellers and provides them to consumers in one place.
  • DistributorA distributor supplies goods from manufacturers or wholesalers to retailers.
  • Public goodsA public good is available to all members of society.
  • FSSAIFSSAI certifies that tested food products meet safety standards.
