Words this chapter explains

  • RepresentativeA person chosen to act or make decisions for others.
  • LegislatureThe part of government that makes laws.
  • ExecutiveThe part of government that implements laws.
  • ConstitutionA book of the fundamental rules of a country.
  • Universal adult franchiseThe right of every eligible adult citizen to vote.
  • AccountabilityThe duty of government to answer to the people who elected it.
  • Separation of powerThe legislature, executive and judiciary work independently.
  • RepublicA government whose head of state is elected rather than inherited.
  • SovereignIndependent from outside control in the source of a country’s power.
  • Absolute monarchyA monarchy in which the monarch controls the making and enforcement of laws.
  • Constitutional monarchyA monarchy in which the monarch has only nominal power.
  • TheocracyA government ruled according to religious rules and leaders.
  • DictatorshipA government in which one person or a small group has unrestricted power.
  • OligarchyA government in which a small powerful group makes important decisions.
