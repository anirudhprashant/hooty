Words this chapter explains

  • empireA large territory made up of many smaller kingdoms or territories.
  • tributeMoney, gold, grain or goods given to an emperor as a sign of submission.
  • guildA powerful association of traders, craftsmen or moneylenders.
  • satrapA governor of a far-off province, left to manage it for an overlord.
  • edictAn official declaration issued by a king or authority.
  • emissarySomeone sent on a special mission, often a diplomatic one.
  • posterityThe generations to come.
  • dharmaMoral law, duty and right conduct towards family, community and country.
  • BrahmiAn ancient script in which many Aśhokan edicts were written.
  • PrakritA popular language of many parts of ancient India.
  • GymnosophistsIndian sages whom the Greeks called Gymnosophists.
  • KauṭilyaA wise teacher and adviser who helped Chandragupta Maurya rise to power.
  • ArthaśhāstraKauṭilya's famous text on the science of governance and economics.
  • saptāngaThe seven parts that make up a kingdom, according to Kauṭilya.
